## Segment table

| Segment | Time | Picture | Sound / pass line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem | 0–3s | A real loss in use: spill, jam, wasted time | One concrete problem; no brand name |
| Proof 1 | 3–7s | Product enters, problem stops; hand close-up | Action sound; the use is visible |
| Proof 2 | 7–11s | Another condition proves it is not a fluke | One condition line; no exaggeration |
| Proof 3 | 11–13s | Measurable result: time, quantity, before/after | A number or comparison; no adjectives |
| Action | 13–18s | Product returns to life, person leaves | One next step; no second slogan |

Times are ceilings. Get one usable shot per segment before adding more.
