## Segment table

| Segment | Time | Picture | Sound / pass line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Verse space | 0–10s | A recognizable space with the person inside | Ambience or low music; do not bury the vocal |
| Verse person | 10–25s | One repeated action establishes the state | Lyrics audible; image does not explain them |
| Chorus motif | 25–45s | One repeatable action or visual motif | Music lifts; action lands on the beat |
| Bridge turn | 45–55s | Change space or relationship | Softer music is not a turn; the picture must change |
| Return | 55–60s | The motif appears again in a new position | Return to the chorus motif; no caption summary |

Times are ceilings. Get one usable shot per segment before adding more.
