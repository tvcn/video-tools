# News Template: Scene, Context, Impact

> Scene, context, impact; each section answers one viewer question.

## Fill three lines before rolling

- Title / client:
- Duration / ratio:
- One shot you must get today:

## Segment table

| Segment | Time | Picture | Sound / pass line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scene | 0–10s | What is happening now, or the latest scene footage | One “what happened” line; time and place clear |
| Verification | 10–25s | Who confirmed, which document, which office | Source on screen; unknowns named as unknown |
| Context | 25–40s | Only one or two images needed to explain this event | No history lecture; one cause line |
| Impact | 40–55s | Who is affected and how | Separate what happened from what may happen |
| Next | 55–60s | One actionable step or a clear uncertainty | Do not conclude for the audience |

Times are ceilings. Get one usable shot per segment before adding more.
