## Segment table

| Beat | Picture | Sound | Duration | Pass line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before | Hands/shoes/breath | Heartbeat/ambience | 8–10 sec | No motivational text |
| Failure | One failed action | Friction/impact | 15–20 sec | Failure can be retold |
| Training | Locked repeat | Steps/breath | 20–25 sec | Repetition not chopped |
| Breaking point | Close-up pause | Ambience drops | 15–20 sec | No slow-mo crutch |
| Result | Finish/timer | Crowd | 15–20 sec | One number |
| Aftermath | Body relaxes | Breath | 8–10 sec | No slogan |

Times are ceilings. Get one usable shot per segment before adding more.
