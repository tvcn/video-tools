## Segment table

| Segment | Time | Picture | Sound / pass line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scene action | 0–10s | The person in the place where they actually work, hands busy | Ambience; no résumé question first |
| Work | 10–25s | One complete work action shows difficulty | Sync sound; interview does not cover the action |
| Question 1 | 25–40s | Sit-down interview, work traces still in the background | Question about the action just seen |
| Question 2 | 40–50s | Reaction shot and hand detail | Ask about cost, not birthplace |
| Close | 50–60s | The person returns to work or looks at the space | One concrete line; no life summary |

Times are ceilings. Get one usable shot per segment before adding more.
