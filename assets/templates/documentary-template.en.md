## Segment table

| Segment | Time | Picture | Sound / pass line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Person and task | 0–10s | The person is already working, not standing for an interview | Ambience first; no explanatory music |
| Process | 10–30s | Continuous action shows steps and difficulty | Keep sync sound; interview only supports |
| Change | 30–45s | A visible result: finished, failed, changed decision | Do not let the subject say “I changed” |
| Aftermath | 45–60s | The first action or space after the change | One true line; no era summary |

Times are ceilings. Get one usable shot per segment before adding more.
