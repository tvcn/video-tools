## Segment table

| Segment | Time | Picture | Sound / pass line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claim | 0–3s | Medium frontal, subject to camera, one object on the desk | One claim under 15 words; no reading; repeatable |
| Example 1 | 3–13s | Hand or object close-up, one complete action | Sync action sound; the image proves it without narration |
| Example 2 | 13–23s | New space or angle, same claim | One explanation, max 20 words; no second center |
| Example 3 | 23–33s | Counter-example or before/after | Do not mock the counter-example; name the difference |
| Action | 33–45s | One imitable action or a single entry point | One next step only; no slogan |

Times are ceilings. Get one usable shot per segment before adding more.
