## Segment table

| Segment | Time | Picture | Sound / pass line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening action | 0–10s | A morning action: tie, shoes, closing a window | Ambience; not a dress still life |
| Preparation | 10–25s | The two people prepare in separate spaces | Little dialogue; no sentimental score |
| Ceremony | 25–45s | The ceremony happens whole, not in fragments | Keep vows sync; pull music back |
| Vow | 45–55s | One face and the other person's reaction | One complete true line; do not cut it into quotes |
| Blessing | 55–60s | The first embrace or exit after the ceremony | No “happy forever”; leave one concrete action |

Times are ceilings. Get one usable shot per segment before adding more.
