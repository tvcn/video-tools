## Segment table

| Segment | Time | Picture | Sound / pass line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claim | 0–5s | A concrete scene, no logo yet | One arguable sentence under 15 words |
| Proof 1 | 5–15s | One person doing one specific thing | Action sound; no “craftsmanship” voiceover |
| Proof 2 | 15–25s | A difficulty appears, the person does not retreat | One cost line; no slogan |
| Counter-example | 25–35s | Another way fails or is rejected | Do not mock competitors; show the difference |
| Landing | 35–45s | The person acts; brand appears last | End on action, not a slogan |

Times are ceilings. Get one usable shot per segment before adding more.
