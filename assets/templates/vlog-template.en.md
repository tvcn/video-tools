## Segment table

| Segment | Time | Picture | Sound / pass line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reason | 0–5s | The person is already moving, not an aerial landscape | One “why I came” line; no epic music |
| Departure | 5–20s | First action: buy a ticket, board, open a door | Ambience; establish the space |
| Encounter | 20–40s | An unplanned person or event enters | Keep the unexpected sync sound; no all-narration |
| Change | 40–55s | A small decision produces a visible action | The change happens on screen, not in explanation |
| Look back | 55–60s | The person pauses and looks at the space left behind | One true line; no life summary |

Times are ceilings. Get one usable shot per segment before adding more.
