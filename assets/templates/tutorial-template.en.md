## Segment table

| Segment | Time | Picture | Sound / pass line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Result | 0–5s | Show the finished result first | One “what you will get” line |
| Goal | 5–15s | Compare result and starting point | State prerequisites and materials |
| Step 1 | 15–25s | Hands operate; key control enlarged | One action per step; an on-screen cue |
| Step 2 | 25–40s | Change one condition, still completes | Name the common error; do not skip it |
| Step 3 | 40–55s | Finish and replay the result | The viewer can judge success alone |
| Check | 55–60s | Freeze result, list checks on screen | Three self-checks; never “done is enough” |

Times are ceilings. Get one usable shot per segment before adding more.
