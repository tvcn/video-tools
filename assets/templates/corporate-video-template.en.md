# Corporate Video Template: One Sheet to Shoot

> A one-sheet corporate video plan: six beats, picture, sound, duration, and pass line; fill it before rolling and run the set from it.

## Fill three lines before rolling

- Title / client:
- Duration / ratio:
- One shot you must get today:

## Segment table

| Beat | Picture | Sound | Duration | Pass line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem | Hands/desk/thermometer | One concrete loss | 5–8 sec | No company name |
| Person | Medium, doing the job | Live sound | 15–20 sec | Job action visible |
| Failure | Repeated action/old tool | Phone or keyboard | 15–20 sec | Cost can be named |
| Intervention | One product/process | One operating line | 20–25 sec | No five features |
| Result | Before/after or testimony | One third-party line | 20–25 sec | Checkable |
| Next | Person leaves | Ambience | 8–10 sec | No slogan |

Times are ceilings. Get one usable shot per segment before adding more.
