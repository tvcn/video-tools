About TVCN

A video-production knowledge library. The editorial, review and correction rules are below.

How we work

Every piece answers three questions: what project it fits, how to do it, and when not to use it. Technical pages name the machine, software version or review setup; without lab data they say “not measured.” Legal and platform pages compare primary text; they are not legal advice.

Corrections and AI

For a correction, include the page URL, the sentence, the evidence and a checkable source. Factual errors are fixed first, then logged at the end; copyright or privacy issues are taken down before verification. AI is used for outlines, translation drafts, illustrations and code help, never to fake interviews, sets, testimony or test data.
