AI Video / BeginnerStart here: which AI video should you make?For your first AI video, do not ask which model is strongest. Ask what you must deliver. Pick a path by output: 30s product, talking head, short drama, localization or training. AI Video / BeginnerYour first 30-second AI videoSeven days is not learning every model. It is finishing one 30s postable video: three shots, one music bed, one subtitle set, one phone check. AI Video / BeginnerThe six parts of an AI videoAn AI video is not one prompt; it is six parts: shot, subject, action, light, sound and edit. Miss one and the viewer says something is off. AI Video / BeginnerReference images and first framesA reference is not a pretty image. It answers who the subject is, the ratio, where light comes from and what the background is. A first frame must also leave room for motion. AI Video / BeginnerGenerate three, choose oneOne generation is a lottery; three gives comparison. Do not choose the prettiest; choose stable subject, believable action and a clip that connects to the next shot. AI Video / BeginnerYour first AI editYour first edit does four things: connect shots, clean dialogue, sync subtitles and export. Do not grade or add effects first. AI Video / WorkflowScript to screen: 12 stepsThe 12 steps are not prompt → generate → edit. Work backward from delivery: script, shot cards, references, routing, generation, selects, sound, QC and delivery, each with an inspectable output. AI Video / ReferenceTroubleshooting: hands, faces, flicker, text, syncLocate by symptom before changing models. Hands, faces, flicker, text and sync have different fixes; changing models is the last step.
