AI Video / WorkflowScript to screen: 12 stepsThe 12 steps are not prompt → generate → edit. Work backward from delivery: script, shot cards, references, routing, generation, selects, sound, QC and delivery, each with an inspectable output. AI Video / WorkflowShot routing: which model for which shotOne project should not use one model. Route by shot type: face, hands, motion, product, text and long take, each with primary and backup, then rank by cost per usable second. AI Video / WorkflowMulti-shot continuityMulti-shot continuity is not the same character appearing three times; it is face, wardrobe, props, light direction and motion direction not jumping across cuts. Lock three anchors before shot four. AI Video / WorkflowNative audio and dialogueNative audio is not automatic mixing; dialogue, lip-sync, ambience and SFX are generated together. Write line rhythm first, let the model speak, then repair in post rather than rebuild. AI Video / WorkflowAI product ad workflowThe first rule of a product ad is no product warp: bottle shape, label, color and material must not drift. Make the product hero first, then generate the scene; not the reverse. AI Video / WorkflowVertical 15/30/60sVertical is not a cropped horizontal. 15s needs one hook, 30s one turn, 60s a complete short story; subtitles, safe areas and covers are rebuilt. AI Video / WorkflowLocalize: dub, lip-sync, subtitlesLocalization is not adding English subtitles. Lip-sync, stress, numbers, cultural references and platform specs all change; lock picture first, then language. AI Video / Professional WorkflowAI previz and storyboardPreviz is not a pretty first render; it validates camera, pace, light and edit at the lowest cost. AI previz only needs to be judgeable, not deliverable. AI Video / Professional WorkflowAPI and batch generationBatch is not a loop. Design concurrency, rate limits, retries, idempotency and budget first, or half the spend goes to duplicate requests and failed reruns. AI Video / Professional WorkflowOpen source and self-hostingSelf-hosting is not buying a GPU. Calculate VRAM, bandwidth, model license, data compliance and ops; running a demo is not delivering. AI Video / Professional PostAI post integrationAI output enters post through five compatibility checks: frame rate, color, alpha, timecode and metadata. Miss one and the roundtrip drops frames, shifts color or adds black edges.
