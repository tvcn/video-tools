9 paths

Start from a jobpages / actions / checks in order

0114-Day Live-Action ShortTake a 3–8 minute live-action short from scope to delivery in fourteen days with two to four people, one camera, and one mic. 02Story, Script & StoryboardTurn an idea into a repeatable line, three scenes that change, eight to twelve executable shots, and one table read that removes explanation. 03Edit, Grade & SoundA reusable post pipeline from ingest to delivery: structure first, sound second, grade last, then a technical check. 04Image HistoryUse four timelines to see which old problem each image technology solved: photography, cinema, sound, TV, digital, and generation. 05Media LiteracyJudge a video in a fixed order: source, edit, context, money, AI traces, charts, rights. Pause before concluding. 06Producing & ManagementProducing is not ordering lunch; it locks scope, money, people, time, permits, and risk into one table, and knows which overrun stops the shoot. 07Release & DistributionRelease is a pipeline, not an upload: master, platform specs, title and cover, captions, SEO, launch, and a forty-eight-hour review. 08Career & ClientsFrom portfolio, showreel, quote, contract, and delivery to repeat work: turn craft into a sustainable client practice. 09Your First Film in 7 DaysUse a phone or small camera to finish a watchable 60–90 second short in seven days: idea, board, shoot, cut, subtitles, review.
Pick the nearest jobStart from what is due this week, not what you want to study.
Work node by nodeEach node has one action and one check; no check means not passed.
Go back one node when stuckMost blocks are a missing deliverable from the previous step.

A path is an order, not a course. When one skill is missing, go straight to the guide or search.
