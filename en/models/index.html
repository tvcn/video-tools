AI Video / Models & CostAI video model radar: November 2026Do not pick a model by leaderboard. Pick by shot type, duration, audio, region and budget. Seven November 2026 candidates, each with a clear boundary. AI Video / Model dossierVeo 3.1: what it is good forVeo 3.1 is built around 4–8s clips, native audio, 4K and start/end frames. It fits high-value short shots, not low-cost batch work. AI Video / Model dossierKling 3.x / 4.0: people, motion and 4KKling 4.0 extends single-pass duration to 30s with 10 keyframes and 4K; Kling 3.0 remains the cheaper 15s multi-shot option. People and motion are its home turf. AI Video / Model dossierSeedance 2.5: multi-shot, audio and priceSeedance 2.5 supports 30s single-pass generation, up to 30 images/10 videos/10 audio references, and timestamp-level region editing. It fits multi-shot narrative and ad iteration. AI Video / Model dossierWan 3.0: quality, open weights and self-hostingWan 3.0 is an API model with 30s clips, 1080p, native audio and document-to-video. It has no open weights; for local deployment, Wan 2.2 is the last downloadable generation. AI Video / Model dossierRunway Gen-4.5: camera control and workflowGen-4.5 is a 2–10s, 720p text/image-to-video model strong in camera choreography, sequenced actions and Aleph 2.0 editing. It is not a direct 4K delivery source. AI Video / Model dossierMiniMax H3: open weights, speed and costH3 is one of the few open-weight 15s video models, with native 32kHz stereo and 768p local / 2K hosted. Its license has regional limits; read it before commercial use. AI Video / Model dossierGemini Omni Flash: conversational editing and availabilityOmni 1.2 Flash supports 3–15s clips, about $0.07/s at 720p, native audio and multi-turn conversational editing. It fits high-volume, low-cost product/ad iteration. AI Video / Models & CostRetired and superseded modelsSora web/app ended on 2026-04-26 and its API on 2026-09-24; Gen-3 Alpha and Turbo are also retired. Export old work, migrate the model, then retest. Every entry carries a date and official link. AI Video / Models & CostCost per usable clip calculatorDo not budget by list price per second.
