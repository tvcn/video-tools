AI Video / ScenarioEcommerce and product catalogEcommerce AI video is not about beauty; it is product truth, a clear benefit and platform readiness. One 15s hero, one 30s detail and three 6s ad cuts share one product plate. AI Video / ScenarioDouyin, Reels, TikTok, ShortsAll four are vertical, but pace, subtitle safe area, cover and duration differ. 15s one hook, 30s one turn, 60s one story; do not cross-post one cut. AI Video / ScenarioCorporate video and trainingCorporate video's difficulty is approval, not generation: brand, legal, HR, product and compliance all edit. Make an approvable storyboard first, then replaceable shots. AI Video / ScenarioEducation and explainer videoKnowledge video must be correct first, clear second. AI fits demo animation, analogy scenes and transitions; it should not replace the teacher on core concepts. AI Video / ScenarioReal estate and spacesReal estate video must not lie: layout, area, light and condition must be real. AI can preview renovation or mood, not make a nonexistent home look ready. AI Video / ScenarioNews and documentaryIn news/documentary, AI may do explanatory reconstruction, never fake a scene. Every AI image must be labeled and cannot replace real interviews, people or events. AI Video / ScenarioMusic video and experimental visualsAI in a music video is not pretty images; it is picture following the beat. Music structure first, visual motif second; rights and likeness matter more than style. AI Video / ScenarioShort drama and multi-shot narrativeShort drama is not a shortened film; it is a hook every 15s and a turn per episode. AI shoots clips, but character/set/wardrobe continuity depends on cards.
