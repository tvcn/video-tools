Glossary / 100 terms

Video production glossary

One hundred working terms grouped by desk. Each has an actionable definition and a link to the relevant guide; where no guide exists, it goes to search.

100 terms

Story & Screenwriting 12

Logline一句话梗概

One sentence: who wants what, and what blocks them. If it takes a paragraph, the idea is not clear yet.

30 sec: Write one line for your story: who wants what, and what blocks them. Cut every adjective; keep only those three parts.

Read the guide

Three-act三幕

Setup, confrontation, resolution. A skeleton, not a law.

30 sec: Mark a second count for each act in a 90-second short. If any act takes over half, re-cut the timing.

Read the guide

Storyboard分镜

Turning text into shootable shots, with size, action, and order.

30 sec: Break a 30-second scene into six frames. Each frame gets only shot size + action, no adjectives; walk through the frames.

Read the guide

Dialogue对白

What a character says out loud. Under every line, there should be something they want.

30 sec: Take one line, cut the explanatory second half, and leave only what the character wants. Read it again.

Read the guide

Subtext潜台词

The layer the line does not say, but the character is actually fighting for.

30 sec: Pair a direct line with an opposite action (turn away, stop, or smile) so words and body disagree; shoot 10 seconds.

Read the guide

Want欲望

What the character must get in this scene. It has to be visible or audible.

30 sec: Write the object or answer the protagonist must get in this scene. If it cannot be filmed, rewrite the scene.

Read the guide

Obstacle障碍

What blocks the want: time, distance, rules, or the character’s own fear.

30 sec: Add an obstacle that pushes back immediately (time, person, or rule) and make it appear within the first 30 seconds.

Read the guide

Conflict冲突

Two valid reasons collide and only one result can stand. Volume is irrelevant.

30 sec: Write two valid reasons and make them collide in one line. If removing either kills the scene, it is a real conflict.

Read the guide

Scene场景

One place, one stretch of time, one change. Without change, it is only footage.

30 sec: Write a scene in three lines: who enters, what they want, what changes. If line three has no change, cut the scene.

Read the guide

Match on action动作匹配

Cut inside an action so two shots read as one continuous move.

30 sec: Shoot A reaching for a door and B catching the handle inside. Cut only the six overlapping frames and check the match.

Read the guide

Character arc人物弧光

Under the same pressure, the character’s choice changes, and they pay for it.

30 sec: Write one choice for the protagonist at the start, middle, and end; if the end repeats the start, there is no arc.

Read the guide

Voice-over旁白

A voice outside the frame. It gives a fact the picture cannot, or one person’s judgment. It does not summarize for the viewer.

30 sec: Write three voice-over lines that add only what the picture cannot show; if a line repeats the image, cut it and put the information into action.

Read the guide

Shooting 18

Exposure triangle曝光三角

Aperture, shutter, and ISO trade against each other. Protect the one you cannot lose.

30 sec: Lock the frame, change aperture, shutter, and ISO one stop each, shoot three frames, and note which blows out or blurs first.

Read the guide

Aperture光圈

Controls light and depth of field. Wider means a softer background.

30 sec: Shoot the same medium shot at f/2.8 and f/8, compare background separation and face depth, and keep the deliverable one.

Read the guide

Shutter快门

Sets exposure time and motion feel. Too fast kills motion blur.

30 sec: Shoot a walking person at 1/50 and 1/250, zoom in on the feet, and decide whether you want smear or freeze.

Read the guide

ISOISO

How much the sensor amplifies light. Higher is brighter and noisier.

30 sec: Raise ISO from 800 to 3200 in the existing light and shoot the shadows; compare noise against the cost of adding light.

Read the guide

Lighting ratio灯比

The brightness difference between key and fill. 2:1 soft, 4:1 standard, 8:1 hard.

30 sec: Keep the key fixed; shoot with the fill off and half on, and check whether the face still reads in shadow.

Read the guide

Key light主光

The light that shapes the face and sets the direction.

30 sec: Use one light, move it 45 degrees around the subject three times, shoot 10 seconds at each, and keep the best nose shadow.

Read the guide

Color temperature色温

How warm or cool a light is, in kelvin. Keep it consistent within a film.

30 sec: Shoot the same white paper under window light and tungsten, set 5600K and 3200K manually, and compare which white is true.

Read the guide

White balance白平衡

Keeping white white. Match skin first, then style.

30 sec: Fill one third of the frame with a gray card for five seconds, click-white in post, and compare with auto white balance.

Read the guide

180-degree line180° 线

An imaginary line between two people. Keep the camera on one side so the room stays clear.

30 sec: Shoot a two-person talk with A left and B right; keep the camera on the same side and cut the scene to check eyelines.

Read the guide

Camera position机位

Where the camera sits relative to the subject. Moving it changes the audience’s relationship.

30 sec: Shoot the same scene at eye level, 30 degrees down, and 30 degrees up; keep the one that shows power, not the prettiest.

Read the guide

Set dressing场景陈设

Props, wear, and practical light that show who lived here, what just happened, and what is hidden.

30 sec: Remove three objects unrelated to the character's want, shoot the same beat again, and see whether the information clears.

Read the guide

Camera movement运镜

A motivated camera move: follow, reveal, or pressure. One per shot.

30 sec: Shoot the same action locked off, following, and panning for 10 seconds each; keep one only if it reveals change sooner.

Read the guide

Continuity log场记单

The record of shot, take, and status. Without it, the edit cannot find anything.

30 sec: After each take, log shot number, take, OK/NG, and whether sound was recorded separately; check the log before wrap.

Read the guide

Dual-system sound双系统录音

Picture and sound recorded separately, synced later with a clap or transient.

30 sec: Clap in frame so camera and recorder both catch it; align waveforms in post and re-sync if drift exceeds two frames.

Read the guide

Continuity supervisor场记

The person tracking wardrobe, props, screen direction, and light so the takes cut together.

30 sec: Assign one person to continuity only; after each take, call out costume, props, and position; if any breaks, reshoot.

Read the guide

Camera height机位高度

How high the lens sits relative to the subject’s eyes. It sets who is big before it sets the shot size.

30 sec: Shoot the same person at knee, eye, and overhead height for 10 seconds each; keep one that neither demeans nor glorifies.

Read the guide

Focal length焦距

The lens decides how close the audience stands. Wide opens space; long presses the person into the background.

30 sec: Shoot the same medium shot at 24mm and 85mm with the face the same size; compare background compression and keep what fits the scene.

Read the guide

Shot size景别

The distance between viewer and person: wide for situation, full for action, medium for hands, close for hesitation, detail for one piece of information.

30 sec: Shoot wide, medium, close, and detail of one scene, cut 10 seconds, and delete any size that brings no new information.

Read the guide

Post 18

Rough cut粗剪

Assemble the film so it can be watched end to end, exposing structural holes.

30 sec: Ignore effects and cut the scene to 45 seconds by goal-obstacle-result. If it runs long, remove events, not breaths.

Read the guide

Fine cut细剪

Refine performance, breath, pacing, and transitions. Structure is no longer moving.

30 sec: Take an eight-second passage and inspect every cut frame by frame; ask only whether the audience needs new information at that frame.

Read the guide

Cut point剪辑点

The frame where two shots meet. Put it where the audience wants the next second.

30 sec: Cut the same action two frames before and two frames after; compare which is less visible, then choose mid-action.

Read the guide

J-cut / L-cutJ-cut / L-cut

Sound enters early or leaves late, softening the picture cut.

30 sec: Make two versions, one with sound 12 frames early and one with picture 12 frames early; keep the one that explains the next scene sooner.

Read the guide

Proxy代理文件

A low-bitrate copy for smooth editing. Delivery still uses the original.

30 sec: Generate 1080p proxies from 4K, cut 30 seconds, then relink to originals before export and check for drift.

Read the guide

Scopes示波器

Waveform, vectorscope, and other objective reads. Check them before trusting the feeling.

30 sec: Open the waveform, find the brightest and darkest points, and pull highlights above 100 IRE back; do not eyeball it.

Read the guide

LUTLUT

A color lookup table. A starting point, not a magic filter.

30 sec: Apply three LUTs to one shot, grab a frame from each, compare side by side, and keep only the one that preserves skin tone.

Read the guide

Primary grade一级调色

Balance exposure, white, and contrast so one scene looks like one day.

30 sec: Adjust only exposure, white balance, and contrast, with no local masks; match the scene first, style second.

Read the guide

Secondary grade二级调色

Change one color or one area without touching the whole frame.

30 sec: Use a mask to change one area of the face only; toggle it off and confirm you are not hiding an exposure error.

Read the guide

Mix混音

Layer dialogue, room, music, and effects into one listenable track.

30 sec: Mute music and keep dialogue plus ambience; audition dialogue at -12 LUFS, then add music only up to the point it does not mask words.

Read the guide

Subtitles字幕

Deliver the meaning to the eye before the ear falls behind. One thought per line, two lines max.

30 sec: Subtitle a 30-second clip with no more than 16 characters per line; watch muted and check whether the action still reads.

Read the guide

Loudness (LUFS)响度

Platforms measure overall loudness in LUFS. Peak level is not loudness.

30 sec: Measure a 30-second delivery clip with a loudness meter; target -14 LUFS and redo if it is off by more than 1 LU.

Read the guide

FoleyFoley

Footsteps, cloth, and contact sounds performed and recorded in a studio.

30 sec: Foley only three actions: steps, cloth, and a set-down; mute production sound and check whether it feels more believable.

Read the guide

Dialogue first对白优先

Make the voice clear first; music and effects move aside.

30 sec: Build the dialogue track first, then add music and ambience; drop music 3 dB whenever one line becomes unclear.

Read the guide

Cutting point接点

The frame where two shots meet. Action, eye line, or sound carries the viewer across.

30 sec: Find the least visible cut between two shots, move it three frames earlier and later, and keep the version where the cut disappears.

Read the guide

Dialogue mix对白混音

Placing dialogue, music, and room tone at their proper levels. Dialogue stands first.

30 sec: Export the dialogue track alone, check sibilance and plosives, then cut 2 dB in a narrow band around 2 kHz and compare.

Read the guide

Room tone环境音

Thirty seconds of a space with no action. It stitches shots when dialogue breaks.

30 sec: Record 30 seconds of room tone at each location; place it under dialogue gaps 18 dB below dialogue and check whether scenes connect.

Read the guide

Transition转场

How two scenes connect. Answer how long and where first, then decide whether an effect is needed.

30 sec: Cut a hard-cut version first; add a dissolve only when information needs a time jump, otherwise keep the hard cut.

Read the guide

AI Video 11

First frame首帧

The starting image for image-to-video. It locks framing, light, and character.

30 sec: Generate four first frames from the same prompt; keep only a composition that can lead into the next action, or regenerate.

Read the guide

Last frame尾帧

Where the motion must land. Lock the ends and the middle has a direction.

30 sec: Freeze the last frame as an image and work backward to the first; if they match, the shot has no movement, so reset it.

Read the guide

Character consistency角色一致性

Keeping the same face, wardrobe, and space across shots.

30 sec: Generate three angles of one character with the same reference and seed; discard if the face changes more than 10 percent.

Read the guide

Scene anchor场景锚点

One sentence that locks door, window, table, and light direction; carry it into every new shot.

30 sec: Give the scene one fixed object (door sign or table corner), include it in every shot, and cut them together to test the space.

Read the guide

Reference image参考图

A fixed sample for the model. One clean front view beats ten noisy references.

30 sec: Run two images from one reference, changing only action in one and only light in the other; see which instruction actually lands.

Read the guide

Seed种子

The starting point for randomness. Fix it to compare two variables.

30 sec: Fix the seed, generate four images, and record the seed; change one word only and check whether the change is controllable.

Read the guide

Digital human数字人

A presenter built from a real performance or generation. Consent and disclosure come before publishing.

30 sec: Have the digital human read a 15-second real script; check lips, blinks, and stress. If one of three feels fake, downgrade to voice-over.

Read the guide

Image-to-video图生视频

Generate motion from a still. Best when the frame is already decided.

30 sec: Generate five seconds from one still with a slight push-in only; if there are more than two actions, split the shot.

Read the guide

Text-to-video文生视频

Generate from text alone. Less control, more rework. Write the action first.

30 sec: Write one subject, one action, one environment, and generate five seconds; if a third subject appears, cut words and rerun.

Read the guide

Consent授权

Permission to use likeness, voice, and performance. A verbal yes is not enough.

30 sec: Get written consent before face-swapping or voice-cloning a real person; without it, switch to a fictional character.

Read the guide

AI disclosureAI 标注

Tell the audience when an image is generated or altered. Do not let it pose as a record.

30 sec: Add “AI-generated/assisted” to the caption or end card, name the part, and never label it “fully shot.”

Read the guide

Animation 10

Key frame关键帧

The essential poses. Set the keys, then fill the in-betweens.

30 sec: Draw only three key frames for a 12-frame action: start, extreme, settle; play at 12 fps and check whether the pose reads.

Read the guide

In-between中间帧

The drawings between keys. They decide whether the action feels light or heavy.

30 sec: Add one in-between between keys and check the motion; add another only if it still breaks, never fill all at once.

Read the guide

Spacing时间间距

Distance between consecutive drawings. Same path, different spacing, different weight.

30 sec: Animate the same action with slow-in/fast-out and with even spacing, 12 frames each; keep the one that obeys gravity.

Read the guide

Lip sync口型

Land the closed consonants, then open on vowels. Four mouth shapes cover most dialogue.

30 sec: Match six mouth shapes to 10 seconds of dialogue, vowels first, then consonant closures; redo if off by two frames.

Read the guide

Cycle循环

An action that loops cleanly: walk, run, breathe.

30 sec: Build a 24-frame cycle with two overlapping frames at each end and play it three times to check the seam.

Read the guide

Blockout灰模

Simple boxes and spheres that lock timing, blocking, and camera.

30 sec: Block out 15 seconds in gray, with no materials; fix camera motion in blockout before touching materials.

Read the guide

Rig绑定

A skeleton that lets the model bend, turn, and perform.

30 sec: Pose the rig in three extremes: arms up, squat, and turn; check elbows and knees for inversion.

Read the guide

Stop motion定格

Move a physical object frame by frame, then play it back. Two seconds a day is normal.

30 sec: Shoot 12 stop-motion frames, moving one object two centimeters per frame; play back and check it feels pushed, not jumped.

Read the guide

On twos / on ones12 格 / 24 格

Twelve or twenty-four drawings per second. Make the action read at the lowest count first.

30 sec: Animate the same action on twos and on ones for one second each; compare cost and smoothness and keep the deliverable one.

Read the guide

Cel赛璐珞

The transparent sheet of early 2D animation. It separated character from background.

30 sec: Watch a cel clip, pause, and count background and character layers; test three seconds with the same count and add any missing layer.

Read the guide

Formats 11

Talking head口播

One person delivers one argument to camera. The first three seconds and sentence density hold the viewer.

30 sec: Write a 15-second talking-head script: line one asks, line three answers; record it and check the first three seconds.

Read the guide

Product ad产品广告

Result, proof, one action.

30 sec: Shoot a 15-second product ad; the first three seconds show the change in use, not the logo; then test whether the selling point lands.

Read the guide

Documentary short纪录短片

One person, one ongoing event, one change. No scripted lines.

30 sec: Write one line each for goal, resistance, and cost in a three-minute doc; if one is missing, shoot an interview, not voice-over.

Read the guide

Brand film品牌片

One person’s choice proves a sentence. The product is evidence.

30 sec: Cut 30 seconds with the brand name covered; if the tone is still recognizable it works, if not, rewrite the images.

Read the guide

Feed ad信息流

A vertical ad inside a content feed. The first three seconds must say “this is about me.”

30 sec: Make a six-second 9:16 cut: action at second one, result at second three; ask three people to repeat it back.

Read the guide

Call to actionCTA

One action at the end: buy, tap, search, watch.

30 sec: Rewrite the closing CTA as a verb plus a concrete next step (e.g. “download the checklist”), delete “learn more,” and compare intent.

Read the guide

Short drama短剧

One location, two people, one information gap, one turn.

30 sec: Write a 90-second three-episode hook: episode one ends on an unanswered question, episode two answers half in three seconds, with no narrator.

Read the guide

Vertical drama竖屏短剧

Drama shot for a vertical screen: face high, hands low, want and obstacle in the first three seconds.

30 sec: Shoot the same scene horizontally and vertically; in vertical, put the key prop in the central third and compare what gets lost.

Read the guide

VlogVlog

One person does one thing in one place: arrive, act, turn, leave.

30 sec: From a day of footage, keep only five moments where something changes, six seconds each; cut waking and walking.

Read the guide

Music videoMV

The track sets structure. Three visual motifs repeat and vary. Emotion before story.

30 sec: Storyboard eight shots for a 30-second chorus with one recurring visual anchor; if there is no anchor, redraw.

Read the guide

Talking-head script口播稿

One person speaking to camera. It lives or dies on speakable sentences, not perfect diction.

30 sec: Cut a 300-character script to 120, with no sentence over 20 characters; read it aloud and cut wherever you stumble.

Read the guide

Tools 12

Call sheet通告单

Who arrives where, when, with what, and who to call. One page.

30 sec: Write an eight-person call sheet with only time, place, contact, and latest arrival; delete any mood description.

Read the guide

Shot list拍摄清单

A must-get list ordered by camera and light, every row with a fallback.

30 sec: List ten must-get shots by scene, each with shot size, action, and OK criteria; on set, only tick the list.

Read the guide

Contingency预算缓冲

A separate ten percent with written rules for when it can and cannot move.

30 sec: Keep a 10 percent contingency on a 50,000 budget, only for pickups or overrun; other overages do not touch it.

Read the guide

Location release场地许可

Written permission from the owner or manager covering hours, crew, power, noise, and cleanup.

30 sec: Before the shoot, confirm the permit states date, hours, shootable areas, and noise limits; if one is missing, do not enter.

Read the guide

3-2-1 backup3-2-1 备份

Three copies, two media types, one off-site.

30 sec: Before wrap, make three copies on two media types with one off-site; randomly open one file to verify.

Read the guide

Proxy workflow代理工作流

Low-bitrate copies plus a relink path for editing. Delivery goes back to the original.

30 sec: Cut one minute on proxies, relink to originals, and check timecode; if it is off by one frame, rebuild the proxies.

Read the guide

Delivery bitrate交付码率

The data rate a platform accepts. Read the spec before chasing quality.

30 sec: Export a one-minute test to platform spec and check resolution, frame rate, and bitrate; if over spec, re-export.

Read the guide

Call sheet update通告变更页

The day’s shared page of times, places, contacts, and changes. Not a formality.

30 sec: Write only what changed, who it affects, and the new time; send it and require a reply from everyone, not a full call sheet.

Read the guide

Budget预算表

Split must-pay, on-screen, swappable, and buffer, and decide in advance what can be cut.

30 sec: Split the budget into fixed, per-day, and per-item costs, mark which overrun delays post, and cut only what does not affect delivery.

Read the guide

Stripboard拆条

Break the script into strips by location, day or night, and cast, then build shoot days.

30 sec: Strip ten script pages into 30 strips, grouped by scene, day/night, and cast; if a day exceeds eight pages, reorder.

Read the guide

Audition试镜

Give the actor an action, not a monologue. Watch for a real response inside the doing.

30 sec: Audition three people with the same 30-second scene; compare only whether they are clear and make you want the next line, not looks.

Read the guide

Delivery spec交付规格

The ratio, resolution, frame rate, bitrate, color, audio, and filename a platform or client requires. Write it into a sheet first.

30 sec: Write one delivery spec page: resolution, frame rate, bitrate, channels, subtitle format, naming; get client sign-off before export.

Read the guide

History & Literacy 8

Daguerreotype达盖尔法

The silver-plate process announced in 1839. Images became reproducible, and alterable.

30 sec: Look at a daguerreotype, note its exposure time and uniqueness; photograph the same person with a phone and compare reproducibility.

Read the guide

Montage蒙太奇

Two unrelated shots placed together so the audience makes a third meaning.

30 sec: Cut three unrelated shots (hand, clock, door) into three seconds and see whether viewers invent causality; then reorder.

Read the guide

Media literacy媒介素养

When you see a video, ask who made it, what was removed, and what it wants you to believe.

30 sec: Take a 15-second viral clip and label each second as fact, emotion, or omission; if no fact appears, call it an emotion piece.

Read the guide

Likeness rights肖像权

Using a person’s face and voice needs permission, including where it will be published.

30 sec: Before posting a video with bystander faces, check each for recognizability; if recognizable without consent, blur or cut.

Read the guide

CC BY-SACC BY-SA

Credit the author and share under the same terms. It is not public domain.

30 sec: Use a CC BY-SA image as a cover with credit, link, and share-alike; if one is missing, replace the image.

Read the guide

AI traceAI 痕迹

Odd hands, text, physics, or reflections. A trace does not prove the whole image is fake.

30 sec: Take an AI image and zoom into fingers, text, and edges; if two look wrong, label it AI-generated.

Read the guide

Platform ranking平台推荐

Completion, engagement, and dwell time decide who sees what. The platform is also editing your world.

30 sec: A/B two covers or titles for one video and judge only by three-second retention and completion, not personal taste.

Read the guide

Viewing distance观看距离

How far the viewer sits from the screen. Closer viewing pulls the framing tighter and kills the wide shot.

30 sec: Set viewing distance at three times screen height, watch 30 seconds there, and redo subtitles if they are unreadable.
