AI Video / Beginner

Generate three, choose one

One generation is a lottery; three gives comparison. Do not choose the prettiest; choose stable subject, believable action and a clip that connects to the next shot.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Projects generating many takes without a selection method.
  • Creators turning three candidates into editable footage.
  • Not for: picking the prettiest single frame for a cover.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Fix prompt and reference; change one variable for three takes.
  2. Check subject first: face/product warping.
  3. Then action: start, middle, end.
  4. Then the cut point: can it connect to the previous shot?
  5. Keep one primary and one backup; archive the rest.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Checking resolution first; 4K does not save a warped subject.
  • Keeping one and deleting backup; no fallback in the edit.
  • Using different prompts for three takes; no comparison.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Choose usable, not stunning. A stable, believable, connectable ordinary shot beats a stunning one that cannot cut. Put the four scores on paper and have three people choose independently; agreement rises.

A backup is not trash. Color, stabilization or frame interpolation can kill the primary; the backup saves the shot. Name each `shot03_takeA/B`, archive prompt and settings, and avoid rerolling next time.

Checks / 验收

  • Three takes share prompt/reference; one variable changed.
  • Subject, action, light and cut point logged.
  • Primary and backup kept with searchable names.
  • The selected take connects to the next shot.
