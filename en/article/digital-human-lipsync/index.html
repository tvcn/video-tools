Digital Human Lip Sync: Consonants First

Align stress before phonemes

Lip sync does not need every phoneme, but stress, pause and sentence ending must land. Lock the voice first, then generate lips; a voice change wastes the sync.

Close-ups read the mouth, medium shots read jaw and neck; a frozen neck makes accurate lips fake.

Limits

Denser sync is not better. Real mouths do not open ten times a second; over-alignment looks like a puppet.

Digital-human lip sync: solve “who it is” before “what it says”

A digital human looks fake not because lip sync is off, but because face, teeth, tongue and breath are wrong. Lip sync aligns vowels; consonants and sibilants expose it. Clear likeness rights first, then run a ten-second test.

CheckWatchFailure
TeethDentition when closedTeeth blur into one mass
Tonguet/d/l soundsTongue never moves
BreathPauses between sentencesOne breath, chest still
EyesLook to camera/sidePupils fixed

Project-level checks

  1. Get written likeness consent with scope, term and withdrawal. No consent, no job.
  2. Record ten seconds with plosives, sibilants and one laugh. Do not record only flat narration.
  3. After lip sync, check teeth and tongue separately; they break first.
  4. Add 0.5 seconds of head stillness before output so the viewer can breathe. Constant micro-motion reads fake.

For news, medical or financial use, add an “AI-generated” label and keep a human review record.

On set

Match p, b, and m first, then vowels. One frame of lip drift makes the voice feel outside the picture.

Do this

Digital-human lipsync starts with consonants, especially p, b, and m; small vowel drift is tolerable.

  1. Align p, b, and m first.
  2. Then check vowels and pauses.
  3. If a frame is off, regenerate that sentence only, not the whole take.

Avoid

Do not start with vowels; if plosives are off, the voice feels detached from the face.

3 checks

  • The lip closure on p, b, and m matches the audio frames.
  • Vowels and pauses show no obvious lead or lag.
  • Only the problem sentence is regenerated, not the whole take.
