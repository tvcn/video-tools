Follow Focus: One Hand Can Follow

A follow focus gives marks and feel; autofocus is not reliable in darkness or fast motion.

What to compare first

  • Gear ratio and throw: the lens focus throw decides the feel; too short jumps, too long lags fast action.
  • Mount and clearance: confirm lens diameter, gear position and matte-box clearance; if it does not fit on set, it is useless.
  • One-hand control: handle direction, drag and marking ring must work one-handed; two-handed focusing loses balance.

When not to use it

When autofocus is reliable, the subject is slow or the lens is wide with deep focus, a follow focus is extra weight. Do not give up usable AF for “cinematic.”

On set

Mark one, 1.5, and two meters on the floor. Have an assistant walk the marks and practice ten follows with less than five centimeters of error.

Do this

A follow focus gives focus changes marks and feel; autofocus is not reliable in darkness or fast motion.

  1. Mark one, 1.5, and two meters on the floor.
  2. Have an assistant walk while you pull focus ten times.
  3. Keep the error within five centimeters.

Avoid

Do not rely on autofocus alone; in darkness and fast blocking it hands focus to the background.

3 checks

  • Three distances are clearly marked on the floor.
  • At least eight of ten pulls stay within five centimeters.
  • The autofocus failure case is tied to a specific condition.
