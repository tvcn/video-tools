AI audio post

AI audio post is not one-click denoise. Order: dialogue repair → ADR → Foley → mix → loudness; check each step, never enable all at once.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Dialogue with noise/sibilance/errors needing repair.
  • Projects needing ADR/Foley/ambience.
  • Not for: cinema Atmos or complex music mixing.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Repair dialogue: denoise/de-ess/remove errors.
  2. Then ADR: replace lines, match lip-sync/rhythm.
  3. Foley/ambience separately recorded/generated, layered.
  4. Mix: dialogue first; music/ambience yield.
  5. Loudness around -12 LUFS, peak ≤ -1 dBFS.

Avoid / 别踩

  • All denoise/enhance at once; underwater sound.
  • ADR not lip-synced; instantly fake.
  • Music masks dialogue; completion drops.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Audio repair order cannot reverse. Denoise then de-ess or sibilance is amplified; remove errors then ADR or rhythm breaks. A/B each step before the next.

Loudness is not “louder is better.” Platforms recompress; around -12 LUFS and -1 dBFS peak is safe. Pushing music to -6 LUFS flattens dialogue.

Checks / 验收

  • Dialogue repair has no underwater sound/sibilance.
  • ADR aligns with lip-sync/rhythm.
  • Foley/ambience on separate tracks.
  • Loudness/peak compliant.
