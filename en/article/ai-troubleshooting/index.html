Troubleshooting: hands, faces, flicker, text, sync

Locate by symptom before changing models. Hands, faces, flicker, text and sync have different fixes; changing models is the last step.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Generations with specific defects needing a quick diagnosis.
  • Teams needing a symptom→cause→fix table.
  • Not for: no sample, only “which model is best.”

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Reproduce: same prompt/reference/settings three times.
  2. Look up symptom: hands/face/flicker/text/sync.
  3. Change one variable: reference/action/light/resolution.
  4. Only then change model or fix in post.
  5. Log failure type into the project library.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Changing models first; the issue is the reference.
  • Changing five variables; cannot attribute.
  • Not logging failures; repeat next time.

Go deeper / 继续往下

First principle: reproduce. One failure may be random; three same-prompt failures are a pattern. Then change one variable or you never know what fixed it.

Changing models is last because it costs most. Try references, motion size, light, resolution and post first; only then switch.

Checks / 验收

  • Three runs confirm it is not random.
  • One variable changed at a time.
  • Symptom/cause/fix recorded.
  • Model change is last, not first.
