AI Upscale: Do Not Sharpen the Noise

Check noise and edges before upscaling. Turning noise into detail is the most common fake sharpness.

On set

Check noise at 50 percent, then edges at 200. If noise becomes detail, lower sharpening or change the model.

Do this

AI upscaling starts with noise and edges; turning noise into detail is the common fake sharpness.

  1. View at 50 percent and inspect noise.
  2. View at 200 percent and check edges for noise-as-texture.
  3. If noise becomes detail, lower sharpening or change models.

Avoid

Do not max sharpening for clarity; amplified noise makes the image dirtier.

Where it works

AI upscale suits clean, slightly soft, low-noise footage. It does not suit badly underexposed plates, heavy compression blocks, text/logos or fast motion blur. It cannot recover detail that was never captured; it guesses. A guessed texture is not evidence.

Judge first: if the plate is already a blocky mess at 100%, upscaling only makes prettier blocks. If the lens is merely soft, it may help.

Upscale order

  1. Light denoise first, then upscale. Do not enlarge noise into texture.
  2. Test at 2x and compare side by side at 100% and 200%; do not judge a single upscaled frame.
  3. Sharpen lightly after upscaling, not twice.
  4. Check text, faces and hands separately; mask them out when needed.

Fake sharpness

  1. Noise becomes texture: the model treats grain as detail. Denoise first or change model.
  2. Edge halos: over-sharpening. Lower sharpening and check high-contrast edges.
  3. Text warps: the AI guesses letters. Do not upscale text shots; reshoot or rebuild as vector.
  4. Plastic faces: the model over-smooths. Lower strength and keep skin texture.

Upscale acceptance log

  • At 100%, no new noise or blocks; at 200%, no halos or jaggies.
  • Side by side with the source, sharpness comes from edges, not grain.
  • Text, logos and fingers do not warp; faces keep skin texture.
  • Log model, scale, denoise/sharpen settings and source resolution; keep both the delivery and the original.

Upscale log

Log source resolution, model, scale, denoise and sharpen settings, test frame numbers and the side-by-side result. Keep delivery and source separate; never overwrite the original with the upscale.

Set delivery size first

Confirm the platform’s final size before choosing a scale. For 4K delivery, do not upscale 1080p to 8K and shrink back. Keep the intermediate file for rollback.

3 checks

  • At 50 percent, noise does not become fake grain texture.
  • At 200 percent, edges are clean with no halo or jaggies.
  • Side by side with the original, sharpness comes from edges, not noise.
