Platform Rules: What Gets Throttled

Platform limits are not magic: reposts, exaggerated promises, engagement bait, and sensitive images get throttled.

3 min read · Field noteTVCN Research DeskEdited 2026-11

Read the community rules before the algorithm

Platform rules are not mystical algorithms. Start with four hard lines: whether image and title mislead, whether music and footage are licensed, whether the category is allowed, and whether minors are being exploited. Each line has text in the community guidelines.

Make a pre-publish sheet: title promise, cover image, music source, likeness release, sensitive terms, commercial disclosure. If any line says “probably fine,” the check is not finished.

Limits

Rules change and enforcement varies. “Someone else posted it” is not permission. Test on your own account with a small post and keep the appeal record.

On set

Check four things before posting: reused content, exaggerated promises, engagement bait, and sensitive images. Fix each item, not just the title.

Do this

Platform limits usually come from four things: reposting, exaggerated promises, engagement bait, and sensitive imagery. Fix them one at a time, not just the title.

  1. Check for reposted or re-edited material; add your own narration, footage, or verifiable material.
  2. Remove absolute and promise-based claims; replace them with something testable.
  3. Drop like-and-comment bait, and mask or explain sensitive images.

Avoid

Do not change only the title; images and claims trigger limits too.

Image note. Covers, diagrams and step images on this page are editorial illustrations or structure diagrams, not photographs of a test performed here; they are not evidence of measured gear performance. Where an image is AI-assisted, the caption says so. Read the test conditions in the text before treating an image as proof.

3 checks

  • Reposted or re-edited material now carries narration, original footage, or verifiable material.
  • Absolute and promise-based claims are replaced with testable statements.
  • Engagement bait is gone and sensitive images are masked or explained.
