AI Music: Style, Rights, Mix

Clear commercial rights before style, and mix it under the dialogue.

Write section function before style

AI music is not “epic” and done. Write the sections: first ten seconds establish space, twenty lift, forty breathe; then style and instruments. Clear function lets music serve the cut.

Check three things after generation: sudden volume jumps, natural loops, low end masking dialogue. Fail one and regenerate or swap sections.

Limits

AI music does not replace licensing. Read the tool's commercial terms; music without a rights chain does not go into client delivery.

AI music: define the rights chain before the style

The risk in AI music is not “does it sound real,” but “can it be used.” Platform, model, prompt, post edits and voice cloning can each create a new rights issue. Write the rights chain before generating.

LinkConfirmRisk
Platform termsCommercial use, attribution, limitsFree tier may forbid commercial use
Style promptDo not imitate a living artist“In the style of X” can infringe
VoiceConsent or synthetic labelCloning a real voice
PostKeep project and generation recordNo appeal after a takedown

Project-level checks

  1. Confirm the current account or plan allows commercial use; save the terms and date.
  2. Do not name a specific artist, band or copyrighted song in the prompt. Describe style, e.g. “1970s funk drum kit.”
  3. Voice cloning needs written consent. A deceased person's voice is not automatically available for “tribute.”
  4. Deliver a one-page music source and license note, not only an MP3.

If the platform terms forbid sublicensing, the client cannot use the track in paid media. Say that before quoting.

On set

Check commercial rights and training sources. Mix music to -18 to -22 LUFS and keep dialogue near -14.

Do this

AI music starts with commercial licensing, then style; mix it under dialogue.

  1. Check commercial rights and training source.
  2. Drop the music to -18 to -22 LUFS.
  3. Keep dialogue around -14 and never let music mask words.

Avoid

Do not use AI music with unclear provenance commercially; a takedown arrives faster than a mix note.

3 checks

  • Commercial rights and training source are documented.
  • Music sits at -18 to -22 LUFS while dialogue stays near -14.
  • Soloed dialogue is word-clear and music does not mask consonants.
