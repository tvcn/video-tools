Seedance 2.5: multi-shot, audio and price

Seedance 2.5 supports 30s single-pass generation, up to 30 images/10 videos/10 audio references, and timestamp-level region editing. It fits multi-shot narrative and ad iteration.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Multi-shot ads, short drama, projects needing character/product/set references together.
  • Projects that need one region or timestamp changed without a full rerun.
  • Not for: 5s single shots, ultra-low budgets, text-only projects with no references.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Prepare three reference sets: character, product, set, each under ten images.
  2. Split 30s into 3–4 timestamped beats with one action each.
  3. Change one region or timestamp at a time and preserve the rest.
  4. Use two audio layers first, dialogue and ambience, not ten clips.
  5. Cost by usable 30s, not by one generation.

Avoid / 别踩

  • More references is not better; beyond what the model can relate, they fight.
  • Five scenes in 30s leaves six seconds each; motion cannot develop.
  • Treating region edit as undo; big changes still repaint the clip.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Seedance 2.5's multi-shot is not automatic storytelling; it gives more references and a longer window. Reference organization decides the result: one hero per character/product/set, the rest angles. If every image competes, the model averages them.

Region editing fits small changes: product color, screen content, a prop, light direction. Face swaps, location changes and lens changes are large edits that spread. Log each change as one version line to know which take is usable.

Checks / 验收

  • References are grouped by character/product/set with one hero each.
  • 30s has 3–4 timestamped beats, one action each.
  • Region edits change one area at a time and are versioned.
  • Output duration matches the actual platform/API limit.
