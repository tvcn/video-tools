Multi-shot consistency test

Consistency is not a single-frame likeness. Across three cuts, check whether face, wardrobe, hair, props and light still belong to the same person/set.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Short drama, ads or digital humans needing one character across shots.
  • Character/set cards exist; verify they actually lock.
  • Not for: single-shot, no-character projects.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Three-shot script: front medium, profile close, full-body walk.
  2. Same character/set card per shot; do not rewrite.
  3. Three takes per shot; pick the best.
  4. Put three shots side by side; check face/wardrobe/hair/props/light.
  5. Have a second person blind-check which shot is a different person.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Testing only front; profile collapses.
  • Rewriting wardrobe per shot; color/collar drift.
  • Inconsistent light; viewers feel fake but cannot name it.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Consistency's enemy is rewriting every shot. A character card is not one image for the model but one locked paragraph for the team; same text and references across three shots leaves only action and camera as variables.

Light direction is the most ignored consistency item. A 90% face match can survive, but left light becoming right light reads as a different person. Compare shadow direction before faces.

Checks / 验收

  • Three shots share character/set cards.
  • Profile and full body tested, not only front.
  • Wardrobe/hair/props consistent across three.
  • A second person names the odd shot or confirms none.
