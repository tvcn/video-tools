Equipment and Production Insurance: What Is Covered, What Is Not

Equipment insurance is not “broken, paid.” Separate property all-risks, machinery breakdown, public liability, and transit; deductibles, depreciation, exclusions, and claim documents decide payout.

Scope

This page covers general equipment/production insurance in China mainland; it is not insurance or legal advice. The policy, endorsements, deductibles, and exclusions govern. Confirm territory for cross-province/border shoots.

Key terms: four risks

Property all-risks: fire, theft, accidental impact, water; insured at replacement or agreed value. Machinery breakdown: sudden mechanical/electrical failure; voltage and maintenance exclusions. Public liability: third-party injury/property on set; employee/contractual exclusions. Transit: loss in transport/loading; bad packing and delay usually excluded. For rentals, confirm the owner’s policy and who claims.

Process: policy to claim

List gear (model, serial, purchase price, current value), photograph/video it, keep invoices. Declare high-risk uses (aerial, underwater, fire, vehicles, drones). After a loss, secure the scene, photograph, report to police/insurer, do not self-repair; collect incident note, photos, police/venue statement, repair quote, invoice, serial, rental contract. Confirm deductible/depreciation before repair.

Common disputes: four denial points

Undeclared high-risk use: drone crash or water ingress is often excluded. Deductible exceeds repair: small repairs may be self-paid, but still report. Depreciation: old gear pays actual value, not new price. Rental liability: who insures and who pays the deductible must be in the contract.

Template: gear list and incident pack

Gear list: name/model/serial/purchase date/invoice price/current value/photo. Incident pack: policy no., time/place, statement, photos/video, police or venue statement, third-party loss list, repair quote, rental contract, correspondence. Update quarterly.

6 checks

  • Gear list has serials and values.
  • High-risk uses are declared.
  • Deductible and depreciation are confirmed.
  • Public liability covers third parties.
  • Rental liability and claimant are written.
  • Incident pack is complete.
