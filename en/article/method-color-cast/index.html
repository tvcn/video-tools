The Color Is Off

Fix white balance and mixed light before color wheels; grading a bad source gets dirtier.

Have a person hold a gray card at the subject position, shoot one frame; move the card to the background, shoot another. Use the same white balance. If the card differs, fix the light before grading.

Find where the cast comes from

  • White balance: white paper is not white in the same scene. Shoot a gray card, then match camera and lights.
  • Mixed light: window light is blue, practicals are yellow, and the face is half cool and half warm. Choose the dominant side, then gel or flag the other.
  • Log/monitoring: the monitor LUT and output LUT differ; it looks right on set and green on export. Use one color pipeline; do not switch LUTs between monitoring and output.

A thirty-second test

When not to hide it with a LUT

If the cast comes from the source or white balance, a LUT only makes skin dirtier. Match the capture side first, then add style in post. Do not call bad skin “cinematic.”

On set

Check white balance and mixed light first. Neutralize with a grey card, then grade.

Do this

A color cast starts with white balance and mixed light, not the color wheels. Fix the source or every correction gets dirtier.

  1. Shoot a gray card or white paper under each light position to confirm white balance.
  2. Turn off or unify mixed sources; gel windows with CTO or CTB so warm and cool stop fighting.
  3. Balance to the gray card first, then grade; correct the cast, do not invent a style.

Avoid

Do not grade over mixed light; the face will go green in one patch and yellow in another.

Boundary

It does not guarantee a story. The template only orders structure; the person, obstacle and cost still need writing.

Alternative

If “Shoot a gray card or white paper under” is not the main problem, start with the basic move behind “Turn off or unify mixed sources; gel windows”. Upgrade only after both hold.

60-second test

Run a 60-second test with the gear you own, focused on “Shoot a gray card or white paper under”, and record one number. If the number does not change, do not add budget.

When not to use

Do not use the template when “Shoot a gray card or white paper under” is still unclear or “Turn off or unify mixed sources; gel windows” is only filler. Go back to the person and the problem.

Three failures

  • Every beat exists, but no one makes a decision.
  • The middle changes location, not information.
  • The ending is narrated, with no visible change.

3 checks

  • A gray card or white paper was shot under every light position.
  • Mixed sources are unified or turned off; warm and cool no longer fight.
  • Post balances to the card and skin is not green in one patch and yellow in another.
