AI video model radar: November 2026

Do not pick a model by leaderboard. Pick by shot type, duration, audio, region and budget. Seven November 2026 candidates, each with a clear boundary.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Producers/directors: filter by delivery spec first, then watch demos.
  • Independent creators: calculate cost per usable second before subscribing.
  • Not for: projects needing frame-level cinematic control or a full virtual-production pipeline.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Write the delivery: aspect ratio, resolution, duration, dialogue/SFX.
  2. Split shots into five classes: face, hands, motion, product, text.
  3. Run the same five-second prompt three times per candidate and log the failure rate.
  4. Rank by cost per usable second, not by a single demo clip.
  5. Check region and commercial terms before scaling.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Using a leaderboard as a purchase decision; its shot mix is not your project.
  • Judging stability from one hero demo; run the same prompt three times.
  • Counting generation price only, not retries, review time or post.

Go deeper / 继续往下

A radar is not a leaderboard. A model stable on close-ups may fail on product macro, hands or long takes. Filter by shot type first, delivery spec second, price last.

The seven November 2026 candidates trade off differently: Veo 3.1 for 4–8s native audio and 4K; Kling 4.0 for 30s takes and 10 keyframes; Seedance 2.5 for multimodal references and region editing; Wan 3.0 for document-to-video and cheap 1080p; Runway Gen-4.5 for camera choreography and Aleph 2.0; MiniMax H3 for open weights and local long clips; Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash for conversational editing, 15s and low cost.

A real procurement sheet has three columns: shot type, acceptable failure rate, cost per usable second. A demo answers “how good is the best clip,” not “will the tenth clip still work.” Run each candidate three times, log face, hands, text and motion, then decide on round two.

Checks / 验收

  • All seven have version, official link, region and date.
  • Each model lists three fit shots, three no-fit shots and two alternatives.
  • At least three runs per prompt, logging failure type rather than a single score.
  • Prices carry a read date; channel prices are not labeled official.
