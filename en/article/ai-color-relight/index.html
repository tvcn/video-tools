AI color and relight

AI color is not a LUT and relight is not brightness. Unify color management, relight by region, protect skin; otherwise faces turn plastic and backgrounds gray.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Day/night, light direction changes, skin matching.
  • Multi-shot color mismatch needing a match.
  • Not for: full ACES pipeline or cinema master.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Unify color management: ACEScct/Rec.709.
  2. Separate regions: subject/background/highlights/shadows.
  3. Change direction, then temperature, then intensity.
  4. Protect skin separately from background.
  5. Scope check: skin line/black level/highlights.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Brightening the whole frame; background clips, face grays.
  • Treating a LUT as grading; skin breaks.
  • Day/night by temperature only; shadow direction wrong.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Relight is hardest on shadows. Day-to-night is not darken and blue; shadows must come from one direction. Models often flip shadow direction and viewers feel fake. Set light position before temperature/intensity.

Skin is the anchor. Background can be stylized; skin cannot. Green/gray skin reads as illness, not style. Protect skin with a qualifier, then move the background.

Checks / 验收

  • Color management fixed, no double LUT.
  • Shadow direction matches new light.
  • Skin line acceptable on scopes.
  • No clipped blacks/highlights.
