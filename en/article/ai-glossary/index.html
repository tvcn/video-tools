Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Readers hitting acronyms in docs/courses.
  • Teams needing one vocabulary.
  • Not for: academic definitions/formulas.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Check shot/generation groups first.
  2. Then post/delivery.
  3. Then legal/platform.
  4. Copy ten common terms into the project template.
  5. Update new terms quarterly.

TermTerm
ShotA continuous run of frames; 4–30s in AI video.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Treating open source as free commercial.
  • Treating 4K as high quality.
  • Treating AI output as copyright-free.

Go deeper / 继续往下

A glossary reduces misunderstanding, not looks professional. Mixing T2V/I2V/V2V causes wrong workflows; mixing open/commercial/attribution causes legal risk. One plain line beats a paper.

Terms change. Today's native audio may be “sync audio” tomorrow; add new terms quarterly and mark old ones. Do not unify for its own sake; reader comprehension is the goal.

Checks / 验收

  • Every term has one plain line.
  • Acronyms spelled out first.
  • Legal/platform terms say “official source.”
  • Quarterly update.
