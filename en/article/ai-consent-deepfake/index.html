Likeness, voice, deepfakes and minors

AI-generated or replaced faces/voices are about consent and labeling, not technology. No written consent, no work; minors require stricter handling.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Teams using real likeness/voice in AI content.
  • Producers needing deepfake labels, releases and minor safeguards.
  • Not for: purely fictional characters with no real face/voice.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Ask whether a real person is necessary; use actors/composites if possible.
  2. Written release: scope/term/platform/modification/withdrawal.
  3. Separate voice release; voice is also a personality right.
  4. Label “AI-generated/synthetic”; follow platform rules.
  5. Minors: guardian consent + no sensitive content.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Verbal consent only; cannot prove after withdrawal.
  • Voice mixed into likeness release; scope unclear.
  • Sensitive AI imagery of minors; criminal/platform risk.

Go deeper / 继续往下

“Just a face swap” is not small legally. China's Civil Code protects likeness and voice; deep-synthesis rules require prominent labeling; the EU AI Act has transparency duties. Without written consent, platform complaints and civil claims follow.

Minors need a separate rule set: written guardian consent, no sexual/violent/horrific content, no identifiable imagery, legal review before publishing. If one fails, do not make it.

Checks / 验收

  • Every real person has a written release with scope/term.
  • Voice release separate; sample term stated.
  • Video/cover/description labeled AI.
  • Minors have guardian consent and no sensitive content.
