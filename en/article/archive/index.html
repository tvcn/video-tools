Archive: Open It in Three Years

Archiving is not dragging files to a drive; it means findable, openable, and editable in three years.

The package includes final project, originals or proxies, fonts, LUTs, music licenses, captions, delivery files and a one-page note. Missing fonts and LUTs means a wall of errors next time.

Archiving is not zipping; it is opening next time

Generate a checksum manifest, keep two copies, one offline; restore-test one package a year.

Limits

Archiving is not every version. Keep the final project and necessary media; store old versions separately or the package becomes too big to open.

On set

Archive three things: media, project file, and a readme. The readme lists software versions, plugins, LUTs, and restore steps; test one restore.

Do this

Archiving is not dragging files onto a drive; it means finding, opening, and editing them in three years.

  1. Archive three things: media, project files, and documentation.
  2. Document software versions, plugins, LUTs, and recovery steps.
  3. Restore one random file to another machine and open it.

Avoid

Do not archive media without the project; in three years you have a pile of unopenable cards.

Judge

Three signals are observable on set:

  • removing one shot makes the scene faster, not muddier
  • skin, highlights and blacks sit inside scope limits
  • loudness, subtitles and file specs pass one delivery check

Failures

These three moves erase the detail you just built:

  • cuts follow the music, not the action
  • style before unity: shots jump in color
  • loudness and subtitles are checked at the very end

Extend

10-minute exercise: Take 30 seconds of footage and make an A/B, changing only “Archive three things: media, project files, and documentation.”. Note one timecode and one difference before keeping it.

3 checks

  • Media, project files, and documentation are all present with matching paths.
  • The notes list software, plugins, LUTs, and recovery steps.
  • A random file restores on another machine and opens successfully.
