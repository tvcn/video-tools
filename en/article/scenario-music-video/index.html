Music video and experimental visuals

AI in a music video is not pretty images; it is picture following the beat. Music structure first, visual motif second; rights and likeness matter more than style.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Music videos, visual EPs, stage backdrops, experimental shorts.
  • Visual changes by section/beat.
  • Not for: uncleared music or celebrity faces/brands.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Map the track: intro/verse/chorus/bridge/outro.
  2. One visual motif per section, not ten styles.
  3. Cut/speed/flash on beats, not random generation.
  4. Use consented musicians or composites.
  5. Clear music/likeness/location/brand first.

Avoid / 别踩

  • A new style every section; ten videos glued.
  • Uncleared song/celebrity face; takedown.
  • Images not on beat; sound and picture diverge.

Go deeper / 继续往下

AI's MV advantage is cheap style tests, not replacing the band. Test a visual motif in three 5s clips before committing to three minutes; wrong style means full redo.

Rights are the hard gate: music, musician likeness, location, brand logo, even samples. AI does not bypass clearance; it makes infringement faster and harder to trace. Clear four items before generating.

Checks / 验收

  • Track mapped to sections; one motif each.
  • Cuts land on beats/bars.
  • Music/likeness/location/brand cleared.
  • Both 9:16 and 16:9 checked.
