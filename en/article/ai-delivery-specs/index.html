Delivery specs

Delivery is not one MP4. Platform, broadcast and ad versions differ in ratio, bitrate, loudness, subtitle safe area and naming; set delivery before generation.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Producers delivering platform/broadcast/ad versions.
  • Teams needing subtitle safe area/loudness/naming.
  • Not for: one personal social post.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. List delivery: ratio/resolution/frame rate/bitrate.
  2. Loudness: platform -14 to -12 LUFS; broadcast per spec.
  3. Subtitle safe: bottom 10–15%, not edge.
  4. Naming: project_version_ratio_language_date.
  5. Check each version: phone/TV/platform upload.

Avoid / 别踩

  • One MP4 only; platform/client needs three.
  • Loudness over spec; platform recompression muddies dialogue.
  • Subtitles at edge; TV/phone UI covers.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Delivery specs belong before generation, not after the edit. 9:16 subject placement, 16:9 environment and 1:1 centering are different compositions; crop after generation cuts or shrinks the subject.

Loudness and subtitles cause the most rework. Platforms recompress; -14 to -12 LUFS is safer. Subtitles in bottom 10–15% survive UI. Put both in the delivery table and save three review rounds.

Checks / 验收

  • Delivery table lists ratio/resolution/rate/bitrate/loudness.
  • Subtitles in safe area for each version.
  • Naming traces version/date/language.
  • Checked on platform/TV/phone.
