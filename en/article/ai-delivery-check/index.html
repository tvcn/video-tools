AI Delivery Check: Flicker, Hands, Text

Check flicker, hands, text, and lip sync. Any one breaks the illusion.

Check source, rights and disclosure before AI delivery

Log every generated shot: tool, prompt, version, references, rights status. Before delivery confirm commercial use, no real-person imitation, disclosure and traceability.

Keep generated and live media in separate folders; when the client asks how a second was made, answer in five minutes.

Limits

Technical QC is not rights clearance. A clean frame with bad music, font, likeness or model terms is still undeliverable.

On set

Check four things frame by frame: flicker, finger count, on-screen text, and lips. Watch each shot twice, once at speed and once frame by frame.

Do this

AI delivery check starts with flicker, fingers, text, and lipsync; one failure breaks the illusion.

  1. Check four things frame by frame: flicker, finger count, on-screen text, lipsync.
  2. Watch every shot twice: normal speed and frame by frame.
  3. Timecode the failure and regenerate that shot, not the whole film.

Avoid

Do not watch only at normal speed; flicker and finger errors show frame by frame.

3 checks

  • Every shot is watched at normal speed and frame by frame.
  • Flicker, fingers, text, and lipsync are all logged.
  • Failures are timecoded and fixed at the shot level, not by regenerating the film.
