Procurement, contracts, data and commercial use

AI video contracts need six terms: model version, output rights, data use, region, sunset/migration and liability cap. Do not rely on “commercial license” alone.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Teams buying API, subscription or self-hosted models.
  • Producers using AI output in ads/client delivery.
  • Not for: personal, non-commercial trials.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. List use cases: ads/film/ecommerce/internal.
  2. Check output rights: commercial/modify/resell/exclusive.
  3. Check data: training/retention/cross-border.
  4. Write sunset/migration: notice/export/replacement.
  5. Write liability cap/indemnity/termination.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Only “commercial”; no modify/resell/exclusive.
  • Ignoring training terms; client assets enter the model.
  • No sunset clause; model shutdown stops the project.

Go deeper / 继续往下

“Commercial” is four rights: commercial, modify, resell, exclusive. Many plans allow commercial only, not resale/exclusive; using it in a client project may breach terms. Tick each item, not the sales pitch.

Data terms are riskier than output rights. Client assets, actor likeness or unreleased product images used for training can breach confidentiality/likeness. Contract “no training/deletable/no cross-border” and keep deletion proof.

Checks / 验收

  • Contract has four output rights.
  • Data training/retention/cross-border stated.
  • Sunset notice/export/replacement stated.
  • Liability cap/indemnity/termination stated.

Regional note for US / UK / EU readers

This English page keeps mainland-China examples where they are part of the method. Read platform names as examples, not as the only market: Douyin ↔ TikTok/Reels, Bilibili ↔ YouTube, Xiaohongshu ↔ Instagram/Pinterest; RMB figures are not converted here. Model availability, training-data rules, disclosure duties and privacy law differ by market; check the EU AI Act, UK GDPR and your local platform policy before publishing.
