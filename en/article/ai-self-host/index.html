Open source and self-hosting

Self-hosting is not buying a GPU. Calculate VRAM, bandwidth, model license, data compliance and ops; running a demo is not delivering.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Teams requiring on-prem inference and data residency.
  • Projects fine-tuning open models with LoRA.
  • Not for: zero-ops teams or mandatory 4K closed-model quality.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Calculate VRAM: model+VAE+text encoder+headroom.
  2. Calculate bandwidth: weights/assets/output.
  3. Read license: region/revenue/attribution.
  4. Build a minimal loop: input→generate→persist→QC.
  5. Then scale: concurrency/queue/monitoring/backup.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Counting model only; VAE/encoder OOM.
  • Ignoring license region; commercial violation.
  • Demo to client without backup/monitoring.

Go deeper / 继续往下

The hidden cost is ops: model updates, drivers, CUDA, corrupted weights, full disks, dropped GPUs. Cloud hides it in unit price; local turns it into salaries.

Read the license as a procurement term, not a README. Regional exclusions, revenue caps, attribution and banned uses: any mismatch means no commercial use, no matter how fast. Legal first, GPUs second.

Checks / 验收

  • VRAM includes VAE/encoder/headroom.
  • License region/revenue/attribution checked.
  • Minimal loop persists/QCs/reruns.
  • Monitoring/backup/upgrade plan.
