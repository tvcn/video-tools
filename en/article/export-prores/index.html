ProRes is for archive and regrading, not direct upload to every platform; files are large but re-editable.

On set

Archive in ProRes 422 HQ and deliver 422. Do not upload ProRes to platforms; the files are too large.

Do this

  1. Archive in ProRes 422 HQ.
  2. Deliver in 422.
  3. Do not upload ProRes to platforms; the file is too large.

Avoid

Do not treat ProRes as a platform delivery format; it gets compressed back to H.264 anyway.

Three causes

For “ProRes Export: Archive and Delivery”, check three directions first. Change one variable, record a 30-second comparison, and see which link the change follows.

CauseSignal on setFive-minute test
Wrong profileUsing ProRes 422 LT as a master leaves no room for gradingArchive with 422 HQ or 4444

Not enough storageProRes files are large and the write fails halfwayReserve twice the space and verify

On-set diagnosis

  1. Decide whether the file is an archive, a grading intermediate or a web deliverable.
  2. Use MediaInfo to check profile, colour tags and audio channels.
  3. Import into the target NLE and confirm no transcode and no colour shift.

Fix

ProRes is an intermediate/archive format, not a web delivery format. Decide its place in the pipeline before choosing a profile.

  1. Archive/grading: ProRes 422 HQ; alpha or maximum: 4444.
  2. Proxy/offline: ProRes Proxy or 422 LT; never use them as the final master.
  3. Keep source frame rate, colour space and audio channels; do not change them here.
  4. Checksum the export to confirm the file is complete.

Failure cases

With “ProRes Export: Archive and Delivery”, these three moves make it worse. Pick one replacement action and do not change three variables at once.

FailureWhy it gets worseReplace with
Upload ProRes to a platformThe file is huge and gets rejected or transcodedMake a separate H.264 deliverable
Use LT as masterThere is too little room for grading and shadows breakUse HQ for the master
Ignore audioWrong audio channels are discovered in postCheck before export

Judge

This page passes on three observable checks, not slogans:

  • 1. Archive is ProRes 422 HQ and regradable.
  • 2. Client delivery is 422 within the contracted size.
  • 3. A separate H.264 upload exists; ProRes is not uploaded.

Failures

Any one of these means stop and fix it before post:

  • 1. Check 1 is only staged (“Archive is ProRes 422 HQ and regradable”); another camera position or location exposes it.
  • 2. Check 2 is left to post or a verbal explanation (“Client delivery is 422 within the contracted s…”); nothing on set can be rechecked.
  • 3. Do not treat ProRes as a platform delivery format; it gets compressed back to H.264 anyway.

Extend

10-minute exercise: Run a 60-second test with the gear you already own, measuring only “Archive in ProRes 422 HQ.”. Record two numbers: what you need and what the gear actually gives.
