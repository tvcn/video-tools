Software, hardware and budget

The bottleneck is often RAM/storage/network, not GPU. Prevent out-of-memory, lost assets and slow upload before chasing speed.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Creators buying a computer/phone/storage for AI video.
  • Teams needing minimum/recommended/high configurations.
  • Not for: specific benchmarks/mining cards/overclocking.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. RAM first: 16GB minimum, 32GB recommended.
  2. Storage: NVMe + NAS backup.
  3. GPU: 8GB draft, 16GB local, 24GB batch.
  4. Network: 100Mbps upload minimum.
  5. Then monitor/audio: 4K/monitoring.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Only GPU; 16GB RAM dies on a 4K timeline.
  • RAID as backup; deletion/ransomware still loses.
  • Wi-Fi for 4K assets; upload stalls.

Go deeper / 继续往下

AI video stalls in three places: RAM, disk, upload. GPU sets speed; these three set whether you can work. More RAM/disk is cheaper than a new GPU.

Local needs VRAM but also model/quantization/VAE. A 24GB card is tight for 33B H3; cloud is simpler for most teams. Compare usable minutes per month before buying.

Checks / 验收

  • RAM ≥16GB, 32GB recommended.
  • Assets/output/backup separate.
  • Upload ≥100Mbps or dedicated.
  • 3-2-1 backup restorable.
