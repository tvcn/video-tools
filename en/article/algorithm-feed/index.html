Algorithm Feed: Why the Same Thing Again?

Feeds amplify what holds attention; the longer you stay, the more it assumes you like it.

Watch completion before watching the algorithm

The most stable recommendation signal is viewing behavior: completion, dwell, rewatch, comments. Do not treat it as magic; hold the first three seconds, first thirty and the middle before discussing tags.

Change one variable at a time: title, cover or opening. Change three and you cannot tell which worked.

Limits

Do not trade content boundaries for the algorithm. Platforms change; account trust does not come back automatically. Traffic bought with violations is repaid next time.

On set

Browse one topic for ten minutes on two accounts and log the difference. The longer you stay, the more it repeats.

Do this

Feeds amplify similarity based on watch time and interaction. The longer you stay, the more it assumes you like it.

  1. Use two accounts on the same topic for ten minutes each and log the first twenty posts.
  2. Mark repeated accounts, viewpoints, and emotions, then compare.
  3. Search the opposite view and clear interest tags; watch how recommendations change.

Avoid

Do not mistake the feed for the world; it reflects your watch time.

3 checks

  • Two accounts watched the same topic for ten minutes each and logged twenty posts.
  • Repeated accounts, viewpoints, and emotions are marked.
  • The opposite view was searched, tags cleared, and feed changes observed.
