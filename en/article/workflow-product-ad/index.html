AI product ad workflow

The first rule of a product ad is no product warp: bottle shape, label, color and material must not drift. Make the product hero first, then generate the scene; not the reverse.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Ecommerce hero videos, detail pages and ad assets.
  • Products with a fixed shape, label and brand color.
  • Not for: concept products with no physical reference.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Make product heroes: front/45°/macro.
  2. Leave label text to post; do not generate type.
  3. Generate the empty set, then composite the product back.
  4. One selling point per shot.
  5. Deliver 1:1/9:16/16:9 without stretching.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Generating label type; three of ten characters wrong.
  • Product warps with camera; bottle/logo drift.
  • Three selling points in one shot; none remembered.

Go deeper / 继续往下

The AI risk in product ads is not beauty but product truth. A 5% shape change, one wrong label character or 10% brand-color shift gets rejected. The stable flow uses a real/render product plate, AI for set and light, then composite.

If you must generate the product, leave the label blank and composite it; lock shape with references; rotate only 30–45 degrees per shot so the model does not guess the back. Less generation, less warp.

Checks / 验收

  • Shape/label/color/material consistent across three shots.
  • All label text composited, no AI garble.
  • One selling point per shot.
  • Three ratios exported separately without stretching.
