Loudness: Platform Target and True Peak

Deliver to the platform's loudness target and leave true-peak headroom. Loud is not good.

Make dialogue clear at normal monitoring, then match overall loudness to the platform target. Do not raise the master gain to rescue dialogue. Check true peak before export and leave 1–2 dB for encoding so platform compression does not clip.

Dialogue first, platform target, true-peak headroom

Targets differ by platform; check the current spec before delivery. If one master goes to several platforms, export separate versions rather than one universal file.

Limits

Louder is not better. Maxing music and relying on captions for dialogue may grab attention, but nobody finishes the film. If dialogue is unclear, go back to the mix, not the master fader.

On set

Platforms often target -14 LUFS with a -1 dB true peak. Mix to the target, leave 0.5 to 1 dB headroom, and do not slam the limiter.

Do this

Deliver to the platform loudness target and leave true-peak headroom. Loud is not better.

  1. Mix to the platform target, commonly -14 LUFS.
  2. Set true peak at -1 dB and leave another 0.5 to 1 dB of margin.
  3. Do not slam the limiter; go back to dialogue and dynamics.

Avoid

Do not treat loudness as competition; a flattened limiter makes dialogue tiring.

Judge

This page passes on three observable checks, not slogans:

  • 1. Integrated loudness sits near the platform target, not guessed by ear.
  • 2. True peak stays at or below -1 dB with no continuous red.
  • 3. Dialogue stays clear and music or ambience does not flatten the dynamics.

Failures

Any one of these means stop and fix it before post:

  • 1. Check 1 is only staged (“Integrated loudness sits near the platform tar…”); another camera position or location exposes it.
  • 2. Check 2 is left to post or a verbal explanation (“True peak stays at or below -1 dB with no cont…”); nothing on set can be rechecked.
  • 3. Do not treat loudness as competition; a flattened limiter makes dialogue tiring.

Extend

10-minute exercise: Take 30 seconds of footage and make an A/B, changing only “Mix to the platform target, commonly -14 LUFS.”. Note one timecode and one difference before keeping it.
