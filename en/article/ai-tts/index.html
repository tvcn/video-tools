AI Voice: Punctuation Is Direction

Put pauses, stress, and sentence breaks into the script before choosing a voice; timbre cannot fix bad phrasing.

3 min read · Field noteTVCN AI DeskEdited 2026-11

Decide who listens before choosing a voice

TTS is not picking a pleasant voice. Define audience and context: tutorial clear, ad rhythmic, documentary restrained. Control pauses with punctuation and paragraphing; a long sentence flattens any voice.

Check names, numbers and emotional turns; machines often lift the ending and turn statements into questions.

Limits

TTS cannot replace a performance. When breath, hesitation and presence matter, record a human; TTS suits information and drafts.

On set

Break the script into short sentences: 300 milliseconds at commas, 600 at periods. Write numbers out and expand abbreviations before generating.

Do this

AI voice starts with phrasing, stress, and pauses in the text, then voice selection; a voice cannot fix bad phrasing.

  1. Break lines into short sentences, pausing 300 ms at commas and 600 ms at periods.
  2. Write numbers in words, expand abbreviations, and mark stress on key words.
  3. Play it on a phone speaker and check sibilance, tails, and stress.

Avoid

Do not judge on headphones alone; phone speakers expose sibilance and tails.

Regional note for US / UK / EU readers

This English page keeps mainland-China examples where they are part of the method. Read platform names as examples, not as the only market: Douyin ↔ TikTok/Reels, Bilibili ↔ YouTube, Xiaohongshu ↔ Instagram/Pinterest; RMB figures are not converted here. Model availability, training-data rules, disclosure duties and privacy law differ by market; check the EU AI Act, UK GDPR and your local platform policy before publishing.

3 checks

  • Comma and period pauses are audible and sentences do not run together.
  • Numbers and abbreviations read correctly and stress lands on key words.
  • On a phone speaker, sibilance is not harsh, tails are not swallowed, and stress is right.
