Cross-border Production Invoices and Tax: Contract, Invoice, Withholding

Cross-border projects stall on “invoices”: a proforma invoice is not a tax invoice. Place of service, receiving entity, permanent establishment, and withholding decide the tax. Confirm the path before quoting.

Scope

This page covers general billing/invoicing for China mainland producers serving cross-border film/ad projects; it is not tax or legal advice. Rates, exemption/zero-rating, withholding, and permanent establishment depend on facts; a CPA/tax adviser must review.

Key terms: five decisions

1. Entity: domestic producer or offshore/HK entity decides invoicing and tax. 2. Place of service: shoot/post inside or outside China; offshore may qualify for exemption/zero-rating with filing and evidence. 3. PE: a long-term shoot/fixed place may create a permanent establishment. 4. Invoice: VAT special/general, proforma, and commercial invoices serve different purposes; a proforma does not replace a tax invoice. 5. Withholding: an offshore client paying a domestic producer may withhold VAT/income tax; state tax-inclusive/exclusive and who bears it.

Process: ask tax before quoting

Make a one-page fact sheet: client location, contracting entity, place of service, crew/gear export, currency, tax inclusive, withholding, PE. Have tax confirm invoice type, rate/exemption filing, and contract terms. Include tax, transfer fees, and withholding in the quote; do not quote on “the client says no tax.”

Common disputes: four blockers

“Proforma first”: fine for quote/deposit, not for tax accounting. “Offshore service is exempt”: needs contract, travel records, deliverables, and payment evidence; not automatic. “Client withholds, we earn less”: if the contract is silent, it comes out of the producer’s margin. “HK company, no tax”: management and place of service matter; changing the payee is not enough.

Template: six cross-border clauses

Scope and place of delivery; entity and bank account; currency and FX; tax-inclusive/exclusive and withholding; invoice type and timing; tax/customs/visa/ATA responsibility. Attach the fact sheet and tax confirmation email.

Regional note for US / UK / EU readers

This English page keeps mainland-China examples where they are part of the method. Read platform names as examples, not as the only market: Douyin ↔ TikTok/Reels, Bilibili ↔ YouTube, Xiaohongshu ↔ Instagram/Pinterest; RMB figures are not converted here. Film-history examples are kept in their original market context; distribution and rights clearance still follow your local law.

6 checks

  • Entity and place of service are written.
  • Invoice type/rate confirmed.
  • Tax inclusive/exclusive and withholding are written.
  • Transfer fees are in the quote.
  • Exemption/zero-rating has evidence.
  • PE/visa/ATA assessed.
