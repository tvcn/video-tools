Platform specs

Platform specs are not permanent; this page gives November 2026 recommendations: 9:16 1080×1920, 15/30/60s, H.264, -14 LUFS, bottom 15% safe.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • One piece distributed to multiple platforms.
  • Ad/ecommerce/DTC delivery specs.
  • Not for: broadcast/cinema masters (follow broadcaster/client spec).

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Pick master: 9:16 or 16:9.
  2. Make platform versions: ratio/duration/bitrate.
  3. Re-layout subtitles per safe area.
  4. Unify loudness around -14 LUFS.
  5. Upload-test each version, not only local playback.

Avoid / 别踩

  • One cut for all; UI covers subtitles/subject.
  • Too low bitrate; blurry after upload.
  • Too loud; platform compression distorts.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Platform specs are recommendations, not permanent law. Platforms change UI, duration and bitrate; this page is dated 2026-11. Check official creator pages before delivery.

The stable approach is master + derivatives: master keeps maximum information; derivatives re-layout ratio/safe area/duration. Do not rename one file and upload; viewers see cropped subjects and covered subtitles.

Checks / 验收

  • Master and platform versions separated, ratio correct.
  • Subtitles in bottom 15% safe.
  • Loudness around -14 LUFS.
  • Each version upload-tested.
