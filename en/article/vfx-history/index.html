VFX History: From Glass Paintings to CG

VFX moved from glass paintings and miniatures to CG; tools changed, the problem stayed: make the fake believable.

What changed

Effects moved from glass paintings, miniatures and optical compositing to digital matte painting, CG and real-time engines. The tools changed; the core did not: shoot a believable plate, then make the composite obey the same light, shadow, haze and lens.

How to use it now

  • Record lens, height, aperture, white balance and on-set light for the plate; the composite needs evidence.
  • For CG integration, match black level, light direction and haze before detailing materials.
  • When studying effects history, read craft, cost and audience expectation together, not a list of “first CG shots.”

Do not misread

Do not bury a wrong plate under more layers. If the plate is wrong, a finer composite only looks faker.
