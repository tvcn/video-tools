The short answer / 一句话

Corporate video's difficulty is approval, not generation: brand, legal, HR, product and compliance all edit. Make an approvable storyboard first, then replaceable shots.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Corporate promos, training, internal comms and recruiting.
  • Projects with brand/legal/HR approval loops.
  • Not for: personal films with no brand constraints.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Write an approval storyboard: one VO line, one image per shot.
  2. Use post templates for brand color/type/logo; never generate.
  3. Use consented employees or labeled AI composites.
  4. Leave replaceable layers: product/data/person.
  5. Generate/replace after approval, not during review.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Generating logo/brand type; typos/warp.
  • Using unconsented employee likeness; HR/legal risk.
  • Generating during review; every round reworks.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Corporate films die in round five, not because the film is bad but because every round changes picture. Split storyboard, VO and brand elements into three layers: lock storyboard, allow VO edits, replace brand in post; approval rounds halve.

AI fits b-roll, sets and concept demos, not real staff/products/data. Use real footage or post for truth, AI for environment and transitions; faster and legal-safe.

Checks / 验收

  • Storyboard approved before generation.
  • Brand elements from post templates.
  • People have consent/label records.
  • Both 16:9 and 9:16 checked.
