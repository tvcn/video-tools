The Platform Rejected It

Check images, text, music, and claims; fix one cause, not the whole film.

3 min read · Field noteTVCN EditorialEdited 2026-11

Check four categories first

  • Content class: violence, medical, financial, minors, politics and news each carry extra documentation. Read the platform's current rule, not last year's experience.
  • Claims and proof: efficacy, price, comparison, expert identity. Delete absolute claims without evidence.
  • Music and assets: commercial license or platform-only use? “Others use it” is not a license.
  • People and privacy: likeness, voice, address, plate, minors. Get consent, or obscure and remove.

A ten-minute pre-check

Watch the cut muted for visual risk; then listen with eyes closed for sound and claims. Compare each item with the current rule and write three columns: issue, evidence, fix.

When not to re-upload repeatedly

Uploading the same version again does not change the result and may trigger risk controls. Get the specific reason, add evidence or change the frame, then appeal. “I think it's fine” is not a fix.

On set

Check images, text, music, and claims. Fix one item, not the whole film.

Do this

A platform rejection: check image, text, music, and claims. Change one thing; do not rebuild the film.

  1. List four likely triggers: sensitive imagery, absolute claims, unlicensed music, engagement bait.
  2. Change one item at a time and re-upload to test.
  3. Keep the rejection notice and screenshots; appeal with evidence if needed.

Avoid

Do not delete the whole film at once; locate the trigger first.

3 checks

  • Four triggers were checked one by one: sensitive imagery, absolute claims, music, engagement bait.
  • One item was changed per upload test.
  • The rejection notice and screenshots are kept for an appeal.
