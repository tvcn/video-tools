Buying Camera Cards: VPG, Sustained Write, and Heat

A card’s peak read speed is irrelevant; sustained write decides whether frames drop. Match the camera’s minimum sustained write, buy one step above, and rotate at least two cards.

First decision: which link is missing

Check the camera manual for minimum card class and bitrate; “shoots 8K” is not a standard. CFexpress Type B suits high bitrate. SD UHS-II handles 4K 10-bit but sustained write varies; V90 is steadier than V60, but confirm the camera uses it.

Fits:

  • High-bitrate 4K/6K/8K
  • Multicam/long takes
  • Fast offload

Does not fit:

  • Light 1080p only
  • Camera only supports UHS-I
  • One card as the only backup

Test protocol: reproducible, no invented scores

Record a full card at the project’s highest bitrate and check for dropped frames/errors. Record 30 minutes and feel the card temperature. Time a 100GB offload. Short clips hide heat issues that appear after 20 minutes.

Alternatives and upgrades: do not add money on the wrong axis

  • Two 128GB cards rotated
  • Rent fast cards for high bitrate
  • External SSD instead of some cards

Buying steps: bring footage, not spec sheets

Run these in order before paying. If one fails, do not tell yourself post will fix it.

  1. Check camera minimum class
  2. Full card at max bitrate
  3. 30-min heat check
  4. Time 100GB offload
  5. Confirm reader and warranty

Check

  • Card meets camera minimum.
  • Full card records without drops.
  • Capacity covers 1–2 hours per camera.
  • No heat warning in 30 min.
  • At least two cards rotated.
  • Recovery/warranty exists.

Regional note for US / UK / EU readers

This English page keeps mainland-China examples where they are part of the method. Read platform names as examples, not as the only market: Douyin ↔ TikTok/Reels, Bilibili ↔ YouTube, Xiaohongshu ↔ Instagram/Pinterest; RMB figures are not converted here. Voltage, plug, warranty, import duty and radio certification differ by market; check the local spec sheet before ordering gear or batteries.
