Backup Three-Piece Kit

Backup kit: reader, SSD, hard drive; offload, verify, and move offsite the same day.

3-2-1 is the floor, not a slogan

Three copies, two media types, one off-site. RAID is not backup; a sync folder is not backup either. Deletion syncs, and ransomware encrypts the sync target too.

  • Minimum: one working SSD, one on-set drive, one drive or cloud copy taken away after the shoot.
  • Working: SSD working disk, NAS or two-drive backup, offline drive; add LTO or cold cloud for critical projects.
  • Team: checksums, version naming, restore log, media rotation; anyone can find and open the original footage a month later.

Three things after a copy

  1. Name by project/date/camera; do not carry “New Folder” into post.
  2. Generate checksums or at least compare file count and total size; do not trust a progress bar.
  3. Pick one random file, open it from the backup media and play ten seconds.

Restore drill

Once a month, restore one random project to an empty machine and record the time and failure points. A backup without a restore drill is only reassurance.

On set

Card reader, SSD, and hard drive. Copy, verify, and move offsite the same day. Confirm three copies before formatting.

Avoid

Do not format the card after one copy; at that moment you have no backup.

3 checks

  • SSD and hard drive each hold a copy with matching file counts.
  • Verification happens the same day and is logged.
  • Three copies exist before formatting, one offsite.
