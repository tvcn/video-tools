AI Subtitles: Fix Names and Numbers

Fix names, numbers, and jargon before timing; automatic transcription fails there most.

Proofread names, numbers and negation first

AI transcription is good on common words and weak on names, numbers, negation and accents. Let it draft, then check every line against the audio; do not publish directly.

Focus on people, brands, amounts, dates, “not” and “no.” One error makes the audience doubt the whole film.

Limits

Auto captions do not replace translation. Across languages, machine word order and terminology still need a human; wrong captions hurt more than none.

On set

Build a glossary of names, brands, numbers, and abbreviations. Replace term by term, then shift the whole timeline by 0.2 seconds to sync.

Do this

AI subtitles fix names, numbers, and terms first, then the timeline; those are the common errors.

  1. Build a glossary for names, brands, numbers, and abbreviations.
  2. Replace each term after recognition.
  3. Shift the whole timeline by 0.2 seconds to align.

Avoid

Do not deliver raw auto subtitles; one wrong name or number kills trust.

3 checks

  • Names, brands, numbers, and abbreviations follow the glossary.
  • The timeline aligns so subtitles neither lead nor lag speech.
  • Ten sampled words show no obvious errors.
