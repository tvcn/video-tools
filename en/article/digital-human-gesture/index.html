Digital Human Gesture: Break the Loop

Break the gesture loop every fifteen seconds: shift weight or take the hands out of frame.

Gestures need an object, not a loop

The fake part is objectless waving. Every gesture points at something: here, you, the screen, the door. Write the purpose first, then generate; without an object, hands circle.

Never repeat the same gesture twice in a row; the loop is immediately visible.

Limits

Gesture cannot replace language. If the sentence is unclear, hands will not fix it; shorten the sentence first.

On set

Design one action every fifteen seconds: pick up a cup, turn, leave frame. Do not let the hands repeat the same swing.

Do this

Digital-human gestures fail when they loop; change weight every fifteen seconds or take the hands out of frame.

  1. Design one action every fifteen seconds: pick up a cup, turn, leave frame.
  2. Do not let the hands repeat the same swing in front of the body.
  3. Watch hands and weight on playback; if it loops, change the action.

Avoid

Do not keep the avatar on one weight and swinging; the loop gives it away.

3 checks

  • Weight or action changes every fifteen seconds instead of looping one pose.
  • The hands leave frame or touch an object at least once.
  • Ten seconds of watching the hands shows no repeated swing.
