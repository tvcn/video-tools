AI post integration

AI output enters post through five compatibility checks: frame rate, color, alpha, timecode and metadata. Miss one and the roundtrip drops frames, shifts color or adds black edges.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Teams bringing AI clips into Premiere/Resolve/AE/Nuke.
  • Comps needing alpha/EXR/ProRes delivery.
  • Not for: mobile-only edits.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Unify frame rate/resolution/timecode.
  2. Confirm color: Rec.709/ACEScct, no double conversion.
  3. Check alpha: edges/transparency/black edges.
  4. Use ProRes/EXR intermediates, not repeated H.264.
  5. Frame-match after roundtrip; no drops/color shift.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Repeated H.264 transcodes; generation loss.
  • Double color conversion; gray/magenta cast.
  • Unpremultiplied alpha; black edges.

Go deeper / 继续往下

The common post accident is not bad generation but failed integration. 30fps into a 25fps timeline drops five frames per second; double color conversion shifts gray; unpremultiplied alpha makes black edges.

The stable intermediates are ProRes 422 HQ or EXR sequences. H.264 is for preview/upload, not repeated intermediates; every transcode loses detail and color.

Checks / 验收

  • Frame rate/resolution/timecode consistent.
  • Color converted once.
  • Alpha edges clean.
  • Frame-matched roundtrip, no drops.
