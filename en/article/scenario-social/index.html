AI Video / Scenario

Douyin, Reels, TikTok, Shorts

All four are vertical, but pace, subtitle safe area, cover and duration differ. 15s one hook, 30s one turn, 60s one story; do not cross-post one cut.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Vertical content for Douyin/Reels/TikTok/Shorts.
  • 15/30/60s versions from one shoot.
  • Not for: horizontal YouTube, cinema or TV.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Write a 3s hook: action/question/contrast.
  2. 15s one message; 30s a turn; 60s a result.
  3. Subject in upper 2/3, subtitles in bottom 15%.
  4. A visual change every 2–3s.
  5. Separate cover/title/first frame per platform.

Avoid / 别踩

  • One horizontal cropped for four platforms; subject cut.
  • Logo in first 3s; completion collapses.
  • Subtitles on bottom; platform UI covers.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Vertical's limit is width. Two people side by side, a horizontal product or a truck shot falls out. Put the subject upper-center; use depth and action, not fake camera moves.

Platform difference is audience expectation, not resolution. Douyin wants conflict in 3s, Reels aesthetics/pace, TikTok raw/native, Shorts information density. Reuse footage, not the opening.

Checks / 验收

  • First 3s has a hook, not logo/empty shot.
  • Subject upper 2/3; subtitles bottom safe.
  • 15/30/60 each works.
  • Cover/title per platform.
