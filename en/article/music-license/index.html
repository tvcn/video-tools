Music Licensing: Sync, Performance, and Platform Libraries

“I bought the song” does not mean “I can use it commercially.” Film projects must separate sync (recording into the film), performance (public playback), mechanical (copying audio), and master rights.

Scope

This page covers music clearance for commercial video in China mainland; it is not legal advice. Confirm by territory, platform, duration, and paid media; overseas distribution involves local collecting societies.

Key terms: four rights

Sync: combining composition/master with picture; clear with the writer/rightsholder or collective. Performance: public venue or platform playback; the platform/venue may settle, but the producer still confirms. Mechanical: copying audio to DVD/download/master. Master: using the original recording needs a separate license. Ads often need adaptation and online communication rights too.

Process: from track selection to archive

Confirm use (film/ad/paid/overseas), then choose. Prefer platform libraries or paid libraries; save license ID, invoice, and scope screenshot. For a popular original, allow 4–8 weeks and contact composition and master holders separately; do not just contact the artist/manager. Credit as contracted and archive the license with the delivery package.

Common disputes: three risks

“CC0/free commercial”: many sites claim free use but the uploader lacks rights; check provenance and license text. “Platform library”: usually covers use inside that platform; ads/offline may exceed it. “Change a few notes”: substantial similarity can still infringe; adaptation needs clearance. AI music also needs training-source and platform terms; do not assume no risk.

Template: license checklist

Title/version/ISRC; writer and composition holder; master holder; use (sync/ad/paid/overseas); media and territory; term; exclusive/non-exclusive; adaptation; credit; fee/payment; liability; license ID. Do not start without these.

Regional note for US / UK / EU readers

This English page keeps mainland-China examples where they are part of the method. Read platform names as examples, not as the only market: Douyin ↔ TikTok/Reels, Bilibili ↔ YouTube, Xiaohongshu ↔ Instagram/Pinterest; RMB figures are not converted here. Film-history examples are kept in their original market context; distribution and rights clearance still follow your local law.

3 checks

  • Sync and performance rights are distinguished.
  • Scope, term, platforms, and territory are all checked.
  • The license names track, clip length, and use case.
