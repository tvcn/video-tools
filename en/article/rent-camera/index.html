Renting a Camera: Insurance, Deposit, Check

Insurance, deposit, and a hands-on check; film scratches and dead pixels as evidence.

A cheap day rate with no backup, a long pickup and no insurance can cost more than the rental when the camera stops. Ask: same-model backup, early pickup, damage liability, tech support hours.

Rent by downtime cost, not day rate

Test card slots, sensor, audio and battery at pickup; do not discover it on set.

Limits

Do not save rent by taking an unfamiliar body. Learning the menu on set spends the crew's money on your lesson.

On set

Shoot a 4K video at pickup: six sides, mount, sensor, shutter. Repeat before returning it.

Do this

Renting a camera: buy insurance, pay the deposit, and inspect it in person. Record scratches and dead pixels.

  1. At pickup, shoot 4K video of all six sides, mount, sensor, shutter, and screen.
  2. Test recording, autofocus, stabilization, ports, and batteries one by one.
  3. Shoot the same pass before returning and compare it with the pickup video.

Avoid

Do not rely on stills; video proves whether a mark was pre-existing or new.

Judge

This page passes on three observable checks, not slogans:

  • 1. Pickup video covers six sides, mount, sensor, shutter, and screen in 4K.
  • 2. Recording, autofocus, stabilization, ports, and batteries were tested.
  • 3. The same pass is shot before return and compared.

Failures

Any one of these means stop and fix it before post:

  • 1. Check 1 is only staged (“Pickup video covers six sides, mount, sensor…”); another camera position or location exposes it.
  • 2. Check 2 is left to post or a verbal explanation (“Recording, autofocus, stabilization, ports, an…”); nothing on set can be rechecked.
  • 3. Do not rely on stills; video proves whether a mark was pre-existing or new.

Extend

10-minute exercise: Run a 60-second test with the gear you already own, measuring only “At pickup, shoot 4K video of all six”. Record two numbers: what you need and what the gear actually gives.

Check

  • Pickup video covers six sides, mount, sensor, shutter, and screen in 4K.
  • Recording, autofocus, stabilization, ports, and batteries were tested.
  • The same pass is shot before return and compared.
