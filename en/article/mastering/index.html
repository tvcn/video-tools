Mastering: One File to Rule the Delivery

The master is the single trusted source; platform, vertical, and subtitled versions derive from it.

The master is the single high-quality file: max resolution, max bitrate, no burned-in captions or separate tracks. Platform versions export from it; never re-compress a platform file.

Master once; platform cuts derive from it

Name it with date, version and project; keep two copies, one offline. Re-releases, caption changes and compilations all start here.

Limits

A master is not “the biggest file.” Bitrate beyond platform and display needs only costs storage; the goal is no visible compression, not the largest number.

On set

Make the master in high-quality ProRes or DNxHR and name it master. Derive the platform, vertical, and subtitled versions from it.

Do this

The master is the only trusted delivery source; platform, vertical, and subtitled versions derive from it.

  1. Make the master in the highest-quality ProRes or DNxHR and name it master.
  2. Derive platform, vertical, and subtitle versions from it; never edit backward.
  3. Confirm the master opens, plays, and relinks before delivery.

Avoid

Do not use “latest” as the filename; delivery must be traceable.

Judge

This page passes on three observable checks, not slogans:

  • 1. The master is the highest-quality format and its filename says master.
  • 2. Every platform version traces back to the same master with no backward overwrite.
  • 3. The master opens, plays, and relinks on a clean machine.

Failures

Any one of these means stop and fix it before post:

  • 1. Check 1 is only staged (“The master is the highest-quality format and i…”); another camera position or location exposes it.
  • 2. Check 2 is left to post or a verbal explanation (“Every platform version traces back to the same…”); nothing on set can be rechecked.
  • 3. Do not use “latest” as the filename; delivery must be traceable.

Extend

10-minute exercise: Take 30 seconds of footage and make an A/B, changing only “Make the master in the highest-quality ProRes or”. Note one timecode and one difference before keeping it.
