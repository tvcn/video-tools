Real estate and spaces

Real estate video must not lie: layout, area, light and condition must be real. AI can preview renovation or mood, not make a nonexistent home look ready.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Layout explainers, walkthroughs, renovation before/after.
  • 9:16 shorts + 16:9 viewing versions.
  • Not for: fake listings or exaggerated area/light.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Shoot the real space: entry, living, master, kitchen, light.
  2. Animate the floor plan; do not generate fake rooms.
  3. Label renovation preview “AI concept”; keep the real before.
  4. Vertical: one space per shot; horizontal: full route.
  5. Human-check area/price/school-district captions.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Generating rooms that do not exist; fake listing.
  • Unlabeled renovation; buyer thinks it is real.
  • Wide lens exaggerates space; area misread.

Go deeper / 继续往下

The AI risk in real estate is misrepresentation, not looks. Generating a 60㎡ flat as a 90㎡ route can be false advertising. Keep real footage primary; AI only for floor-plan animation and renovation concepts, with persistent labels.

Renovation previews need same camera, lens and light for before/after. Wide before and tele after secretly enlarges the space; that is edit deception, not an AI problem.

Checks / 验收

  • Every space has a real counterpart.
  • Renovation labeled AI + real before.
  • Area/price/school captions human-checked.
  • Wide angle does not exaggerate space.
