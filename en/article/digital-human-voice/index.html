Digital Human Voice: Consent and Range

Get consent and define the range before cloning a voice. Capability is not permission.

3 min read · Field noteTVCN AI DeskEdited 2026-11

Match breath before lip sync

The uncanny part is often breath and pause, not the mouth. Give the voice natural breaths, then align lips; leave residual air at the end of a line instead of cutting immediately.

Check plosives (p, b, m) and sibilance (s, sh); they break first.

Limits

A digital human cannot replace a real interview. Use a person when evidence, emotion and presence matter; digital humans suit announcements and fictional roles.

On set

The permission says whose voice, where it is used, for how long, and whether retraining is allowed. No written permission, no clone.

Do this

Digital-human voice starts with permission and scope; being able to clone does not mean you may.

  1. The license states whose voice, where used, for how long, and whether retraining is allowed.
  2. No written permission means no clone.
  3. Label AI voice in the opening and description.

Avoid

Do not present generated lines as a real quote; that is fabrication, not voice-over.

3 checks

  • The license covers voice identity, use, term, and retraining.
  • No voice is cloned or used without written permission.
  • AI voice is labeled in both the opening and description.
