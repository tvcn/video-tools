Ecommerce and product catalog

Ecommerce AI video is not about beauty; it is product truth, a clear benefit and platform readiness. One 15s hero, one 30s detail and three 6s ad cuts share one product plate.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Product heroes and detail pages for Taobao/JD/Amazon/DTC.
  • Multi-ratio ad assets at 1:1/9:16/16:9.
  • Not for: concept products without a physical reference.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Shoot/render product plates: front, 45°, macro.
  2. Write three benefits, one per shot.
  3. Generate empty set, composite product back.
  4. Composite labels/prices/specs in post.
  5. Export 1:1/9:16/16:9 and check each.

Avoid / 别踩

  • AI-generated label text; one typo kills the asset.
  • Product warps with camera; client rejects.
  • One horizontal cropped three ways; vertical subject cut.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Ecommerce KPI is conversion, not views. First frame shows the product, first three seconds the benefit, last the result. AI can generate the set, but the product itself should come from a real/render plate or labels and shape drift.

Multi-ratio is not cropping. 1:1 centers, 9:16 leaves vertical room, 16:9 needs side context. Compose for three ratios at generation; post only nudges. Otherwise the subject is cut or too small.

Checks / 验收

  • Product shape/label/color/material consistent across three versions.
  • Labels/prices/specs composited in post.
  • One benefit per shot.
  • Each ratio checked, no stretch/crop.
