Script to screen: 12 steps

The 12 steps are not prompt → generate → edit. Work backward from delivery: script, shot cards, references, routing, generation, selects, sound, QC and delivery, each with an inspectable output.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Teams integrating AI into real projects, not one-off clips.
  • Producers/directors managing ten-plus shots and collaborators.
  • Not for: individuals making one 5s demo.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. 1 Delivery: ratio/duration/platform/subtitles/audio.
  2. 2 Script: one theme + three beats.
  3. 3 Shot card: size/subject/action/light/duration.
  4. 4 References: character/product/set/first-last frames.
  5. 5 Routing: model per shot with a backup.
  6. 6 Generate: three takes per shot, one variable.
  7. 7 Select: four scores, primary + backup.
  8. 8 Sound: dialogue/SFX/music/subtitles.
  9. 9 Rough cut: connect and finish within 30s.
  10. 10 QC: flicker/hands/text/sync.
  11. 11 Deliver: master + platform + subtitle file.
  12. 12 Archive: prompts/references/failure notes.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Thinking about delivery at step five; ratio and duration rework.
  • No shot card, prompting directly; generations are not comparable.
  • Finding hands/text errors at step ten; rerun cost doubles.

Go deeper / 继续往下

The 12-step value is moving rework earlier. Delivery and shot cards come first, generation and QC later; a failure returns to the previous step, not the whole project. Without process, AI projects become endless rerolls.

Archive the reproducible package, not the master: prompts, references, model version, settings, failure type and selection reason. Three months later it beats memory.

Checks / 验收

  • Each step has a file or record, not just “done.”
  • Every shot has primary/backup/alternate model.
  • All four QC classes checked.
  • Archive lets another person reproduce a shot.
