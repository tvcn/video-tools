Ending: Stop After the Choice

End after the choice. The sentence the audience supplies is stronger than a closing explanation.

2 min quick read + 6 min deep read · Field noteTVCN Story DeskEdited 2026-11

The ending does not explain the choice; it shows the first consequence. If she stays, show her setting down the bag. If she leaves, show the door closing behind her. Stop after the action and let the audience supply the sentence.

Stop after the choice

In the last fifteen seconds, keep one action and one reaction shot. No narration summary, no second twist.

Limits

An open ending is not a missing ending. The audience must be able to name what she chose; they just are not told what happens next.

On set

The last shot is the action after the choice: put down the key, close the door, walk away. Do not add narration explaining why.

Do this

End after the choice. Do not add an explanation; the line the audience supplies is stronger.

  1. Make the last shot an action after the choice: keys down, door closed, walking away.
  2. Do not add voice-over explaining why.
  3. Cut two endings, one stopping on the action and one with ten extra seconds, and compare.

Avoid

Do not explain the ending; too much explanation kills the aftertaste.

Judge

This page passes on three observable checks, not slogans:

  • 1. The final shot is an action after the choice with no explanatory voice-over.
  • 2. The audience can name what was chosen and what was given up.
  • 3. The ten-second explanation version is weaker and the final cut stops on action.

Failures

Any one of these means stop and fix it before post:

  • 1. Check 1 is only staged (“The final shot is an action after the choice w…”); another camera position or location exposes it.
  • 2. Check 2 is left to post or a verbal explanation (“The audience can name what was chosen and what…”); nothing on set can be rechecked.
  • 3. Do not explain the ending; too much explanation kills the aftertaste.

Extend

10-minute exercise: Write one 30-second scene with only “Make the last shot an action after the” and “Do not add voice-over explaining why.”. Cut every explanation and ask one person to retell what the character wants.
