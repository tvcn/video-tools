Music Video Template: Verse, Chorus, Memory

Verse, chorus, memory. The memory can be one action or one image.

An MV template uses verse, chorus, memory point; the hook can be an action or an image.

A usable 60-second segment table

SegmentTimePictureSound / pass line
Verse space0–10sA recognizable space with the person insideAmbience or low music; do not bury the vocal
Verse person10–25sOne repeated action establishes the stateLyrics audible; image does not explain them
Chorus motif25–45sOne repeatable action or visual motifMusic lifts; action lands on the beat
Bridge turn45–55sChange space or relationshipSofter music is not a turn; the picture must change
Return55–60sThe motif appears again in a new positionReturn to the chorus motif; no caption summary

Fill three lines before rolling

  • Motif: one action the audience can copy, appearing at least twice.
  • Space: where do verse and chorus live, and why change?
  • Turn: what relationship changes in the bridge, beyond volume?

On set

Verse gives space, chorus gives a remembered action, bridge gives a turn. The action must repeat.

Do this

  1. Let the verse establish space and location.
  2. Give the chorus one repeatable memory action.
  3. Let the bridge turn by changing space or relationship.

Avoid

Do not make the bridge just a volume drop; without a visual turn, it is forgotten.

Judge

This page passes on three observable checks, not slogans:

  • 1. The verse establishes where the person is.
  • 2. The chorus hook appears at least twice and is memorable.
  • 3. The bridge changes space or relationship, not just volume.

Failures

Any one of these means stop and fix it before post:

  • 1. Check 1 is only staged (“The verse establishes where the person is”); another camera position or location exposes it.
  • 2. Check 2 is left to post or a verbal explanation (“The chorus hook appears at least twice and is…”); nothing on set can be rechecked.
  • 3. Do not make the bridge just a volume drop; without a visual turn, it is forgotten.

Extend

10-minute exercise: Break down one 30-second reference, mark the timecodes for “Let the verse establish space and location.” and “Give the chorus one repeatable memory action.”, then answer: if one disappeared, would the film still hold?

Check

  • The verse establishes where the person is.
  • The chorus hook appears at least twice and is memorable.
  • The bridge changes space or relationship, not just volume.
