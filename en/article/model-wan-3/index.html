Wan 3.0: quality, open weights and self-hosting

Wan 3.0 is an API model with 30s clips, 1080p, native audio and document-to-video. It has no open weights; for local deployment, Wan 2.2 is the last downloadable generation.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Budget-sensitive enterprise content needing 1080p/30s, documents or webpages.
  • Projects needing Chinese prompts, long-document understanding and stable micro-expression.
  • Not for: mandatory local/open weights, 4K, or fully offline.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Confirm 1080p is enough; Wan 3.0 has no 4K.
  2. For document-to-video, compress to one file within 50 pages.
  3. Draft structure at 480p, motion at 720p, finals at 1080p.
  4. Audio is on by default; clear dialogue and music rights separately.
  5. Local deployment goes to Wan 2.2 or a hybrid cloud.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Writing Wan 3.0 into a local deployment plan; it has no weights.
  • Uploading a 100-page document; the 50-page limit truncates.
  • Treating generated audio as rights-free; generation is not commercial clearance.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Wan 3.0 is often misread as “the Wan line is open.” Open Wan 2.2 and Wan 3.0 are different lines: the former has weights, ComfyUI and LoRA; the latter is Alibaba Cloud API with more capability but no weights. Put “must run local” in the brief and Wan 3.0 is out.

Its differentiator is document/webpage input and long Chinese prompts. Turning a 20-page product manual into a 30s explainer saves one rewrite versus text-only; tables, numbers and brand names still need human checking.

Checks / 验收

  • Delivery is 1080p or below and no 4K requirement exists.
  • Document is one file, ≤50 pages, ≤100MB.
  • Local plan names Wan 2.2, not Wan 3.0.
  • Audio rights, likeness and document permissions are recorded.
