Reflector: Five Surfaces, One Job

The five surfaces are five fill strengths, not five styles; start white, not silver.

2 min read · Field noteTVCN Tools DeskEdited 2026-11

  2. When not to use it

  5. Avoid

What to compare first

  • Size and distance: closer and larger is softer; a small board far away only adds a hot spot.
  • Surface: white is soft, silver is harder and brighter, gold shifts color; black is for negative fill, not reflection.
  • Mounting: confirm stand, clamp and wind resistance; an unsecured reflector outdoors becomes a kite.

When not to use it

When direction and intensity must be precise, a reflector is unpredictable; use a second light with a softbox. Do not fill a face into having no direction.

On set

Start with the white side at 45 degrees on the shadow side. If it is not enough, switch to silver; do not start with gold.

Do this

A five-in-one reflector is five fill strengths, not five styles; start white, not silver.

  1. Place the white side at 45 degrees on the shadow side and shoot.
  2. Switch to silver only if more is needed.
  3. Use gold only for warmth, never by default.

Avoid

Do not start with gold; warm skin is hard to pull back to neutral later.

3 checks

  • A white-side 45-degree fill sample exists.
  • Silver is used only after white proves insufficient, with a comparison.
  • Gold is not default, or its warmth is justified.
