Localize: dub, lip-sync, subtitles

Localization is not adding English subtitles. Lip-sync, stress, numbers, cultural references and platform specs all change; lock picture first, then language.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • One piece needs Chinese/English/Japanese/Korean versions.
  • Talking heads/short drama needing target-language lip-sync.
  • Not for: subtitle-only translation or dialogue-free visuals.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Lock picture: shots, duration, subtitle safe area.
  2. Rewrite lines in the target language, not word-for-word.
  3. Localize numbers/units/names to avoid errors.
  4. Regenerate/repair lip-sync for the target language.
  5. Re-break and re-layout subtitles in the target language.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Word-for-word translation; lines too long to read.
  • Subtitles only, no lip-sync; instantly fake.
  • Numbers/units not localized; credibility lost.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Localization cost order is picture > lip-sync > voice > subtitles. Picture is most expensive, subtitles cheapest; lock picture, then language. If multilingual is known at script stage, write short lines, avoid puns and leave subtitle room.

Do not translate cultural jokes literally. A Chinese pun may be meaningless in English; replace it with the same function or cut it. Subtitles can keep information, not the laugh; rewrite the joke.

Checks / 验收

  • Lines rewritten, not literal.
  • Lip-sync matches the target language.
  • Numbers/units/names localized correctly.
  • Subtitles inside the target platform safe area.
