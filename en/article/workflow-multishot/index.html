Multi-shot continuity

Multi-shot continuity is not the same character appearing three times; it is face, wardrobe, props, light direction and motion direction not jumping across cuts. Lock three anchors before shot four.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Short drama, ads or product stories with three-plus shots.
  • Projects where character, wardrobe or set drifts between shots.
  • Not for: single-shot or pure b-roll projects.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Make a character sheet: front/profile/full body + locked description.
  2. Make a set card: hero image + light direction + temperature.
  3. Write wardrobe as a reusable paragraph, not fresh each time.
  4. Tag motion direction per shot: L→R or R→L.
  5. In the edit, check direction/light before performance.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Rewriting the character each shot; it changes by shot three.
  • Inconsistent light direction; viewers feel fake without naming it.
  • A moves L→R, B moves R→L; the cut jumps the axis.

Go deeper / 继续往下

The first layer is “no jump,” the second is “identical.” A 90% face match is enough, but light direction, motion direction and wardrobe color must be 100%; viewers forgive the former and spot the latter.

Manage with a continuity table: shot, character, wardrobe, set, light, direction, props. One row per shot; if you cannot fill it, the shot is not ready.

Checks / 验收

  • Character/set/wardrobe shared across three shots.
  • Every shot has a motion direction.
  • Light direction connects across the cut.
  • Watch without sound; story still reads.
