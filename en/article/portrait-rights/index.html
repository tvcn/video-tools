Likeness, Location, and Minors: What a Release Must Say

Consent to be filmed is not consent to be used commercially. Likeness, property, guardian consent for minors, and AI face/voice synthesis are four different clearances; a “consent to shoot” note will not save a paid ad.

Scope

This page covers personality and location clearances for commercial shoots in China mainland; it is not legal advice. News/public interest, public figures, minors, and AI face swaps are more complex; have a lawyer review. The Civil Code personality rights chapter governs likeness; check the current text.

Key terms: four releases

Likeness: shoot, copy, distribute, online communication, advertising, adaptation/AI synthesis; term, territory, media, exclusivity. Location: owner/manager permission; dates, areas, business impact, brand visibility, drones. Minors: written guardian consent with scope and term; content must not harm the minor. AI synthesis: training/generating a real face or voice needs separate consent and deep-synthesis/AI labeling compliance.

Process: collect before rolling

Put releases in the call sheet attachment: talent, guardian, location manager. For badges/IDs, separately tick “may be shown.” Ads/paid media add “paid media” and “recut” options. On set, photograph the signed release with the person for confirmation and archive it in the project legal folder. Without a release, do not put identifiable people into commercial footage.

Common disputes: four traps

“Street interview”: public space is not commercial consent; identifiable people in ads still need a release. “Ticket”: a ticket is not a commercial shoot permit; venues often require a separate license. “The child agreed”: a minor’s nod does not replace written guardian consent. “AI face swap”: face/voice swaps can involve likeness, voice, reputation, and AI labeling; an old release does not automatically cover it.

Template: ten release items

Name/ID/contact; date and project; media (film/ad/paid/offline/overseas); territory and term; exclusivity; adaptation/AI synthesis; credit/anonymity; fee; guardian signature; location owner/manager stamp. Attach scope illustration and withdrawal terms.

Regional note for US / UK / EU readers

This English page keeps mainland-China examples where they are part of the method. Read platform names as examples, not as the only market: Douyin ↔ TikTok/Reels, Bilibili ↔ YouTube, Xiaohongshu ↔ Instagram/Pinterest; RMB figures are not converted here. 3 checks

  • Every shot with a person is tagged by use category.
  • Commercial use has written consent with term and platforms.
  • Releases map to specific shot numbers.
