Proxy and Cache: Do Not Fill the Drive First

Keep proxies and cache on a separate drive; a full edit drive drops frames.

A proxy is a low-resolution copy for editing; a cache is a preview file generated for playback. Deleting cache does not lose media; deleting proxies takes the timeline offline. Keep them in separate folders.

Proxies and cache are different things

Put proxies in a Proxy folder and cache in the software's cache directory; clean cache only.

Limits

Do not let a cleaner app delete “media cache” in one click. It may sweep proxies and originals. Confirm the path before deleting.

On set

Put proxies and cache on a second SSD and keep 20 percent free on the system drive. When the edit drive fills, clear cache, not originals.

Do this

Proxies and cache belong on a separate drive; a full edit drive drops frames.

  1. Put proxies and cache on a second SSD.
  2. Keep twenty percent free on the system drive and never let the edit drive go red.
  3. When space runs out, clear cache first; never delete originals.

Avoid

Do not delete originals for space; cache can be rebuilt, media cannot.

Judge

This page passes on three observable checks, not slogans:

  • 1. Proxies and cache sit on the second drive and the edit drive still has free space.
  • 2. The system drive keeps at least twenty percent free with no red warning.
  • 3. After clearing cache, the project rebuilds and every original remains.

Failures

Any one of these means stop and fix it before post:

  • 1. Check 1 is only staged (“Proxies and cache sit on the second drive and…”); another camera position or location exposes it.
  • 2. Check 2 is left to post or a verbal explanation (“The system drive keeps at least twenty percent…”); nothing on set can be rechecked.
  • 3. Do not delete originals for space; cache can be rebuilt, media cannot.

Extend

10-minute exercise: Take 30 seconds of footage and make an A/B, changing only “Put proxies and cache on a second SSD.”. Note one timecode and one difference before keeping it.

Check

  • Proxies and cache sit on the second drive and the edit drive still has free space.
  • The system drive keeps at least twenty percent free with no red warning.
  • After clearing cache, the project rebuilds and every original remains.
