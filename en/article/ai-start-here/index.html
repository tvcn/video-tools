AI Video / Beginner

Start here: which AI video should you make?

For your first AI video, do not ask which model is strongest. Ask what you must deliver. Pick a path by output: 30s product, talking head, short drama, localization or training.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • First AI video; you need one path you can finish.
  • You can edit in CapCut/Premiere but don't know where AI fits.
  • Not for: leaderboard shopping or one-click magic.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Pick the delivery: 15/30/60s, horizontal or vertical.
  2. Pick one path: product, talking head, short drama, localization or training.
  3. Make three shots, not twenty.
  4. Run three takes per shot; send one into the edit.
  5. Watch on a phone before deciding to continue.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Buying the most expensive plan before deciding what to make.
  • Writing a 60s script on day one; completion collapses.
  • Treating AI as one-click; it makes shots, not judgment.

Go deeper / 继续往下

The start page is not a model list; it gets a watchable 15s out in 30 minutes. Pick delivery, then path, then model. Reverse the order and you drown in settings without a postable clip.

Keep three things after the first finish: prompts, references and failure notes. They reuse better than the clip. Next time you change one variable instead of starting from zero.

Checks / 验收

  • Delivery is on one page: ratio, duration, platform.
  • One path selected, not five at once.
  • Each of the three shots has a one-line job.
  • On a phone, subject and subtitles are clear.
