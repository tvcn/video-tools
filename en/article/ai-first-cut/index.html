Your first AI edit

Your first edit does four things: connect shots, clean dialogue, sync subtitles and export. Do not grade or add effects first.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • You have 3–5 generated clips and need a finished cut.
  • First AI piece; get sound, subtitles and export right.
  • Not for: professional grade, mix or VFX delivery.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Connect three shots first; no transitions.
  2. Denoise/fix sibilance; check on phone speaker.
  3. Break subtitles by meaning, ≤14 characters per line.
  4. Add music last, 6–10 dB under dialogue.
  5. Export H.264 at platform bitrate; not only a master.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Grading first; unresolved picture problems get worse.
  • Breaking subtitles by punctuation; lines too long on phone.
  • Exporting only ProRes; upload fails or gets recompressed.

Go deeper / 继续往下

The first cut aims to be watchable, not look like an ad. Fix story first: who, wants what, obstacle, result. Transitions, filters and effects come after; unclear story makes viewers swipe faster.

Sound is the most neglected finish. Dialogue around -12 LUFS, music 6–10 dB under, subtitles broken by meaning: these three make the piece feel finished, and cost far less than a more expensive model.

Checks / 验收

  • Three shots connect without obvious jumps in frame or light.
  • On phone speaker, dialogue is clear and sibilance is not harsh.
  • Subtitles ≤14 chars and do not cover the subject.
  • Export uploads and plays on the target platform.
