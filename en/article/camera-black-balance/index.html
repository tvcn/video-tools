Camera Black Balance: Do Not Bake Noise

Black balance as the camera requires; do not bake noise into the look. Clean shadows give post room.

Cap the lens, turn off all light, and black-balance at the shooting frame rate, shutter and gain. A balance made at other settings does not carry over. Shoot a black frame and zoom in for fixed bright dots or color patches.

Black balance in full dark, same settings

Redo after sensor temperature changes; on long shoots, warm up ten minutes first.

Limits

Black balance cannot fix dead pixels. Fixed dots are a sensor issue; black balance only suppresses dark current. Dead pixels need mapping or post.

On set

Cap the lens and run the camera's black balance. Shoot a dark area and check whether noise drops.

Do this

Camera black balance follows the manufacturer's procedure; do not call noise a style. Clean shadows give post room.

  1. Cap the lens and run black balance from the menu.
  2. Shoot a dark area afterward.
  3. Compare noise before and after to confirm cleanup.

Avoid

Do not skip black balance after conditions change; shadow noise drifts with temperature.

Judge

This page passes on three observable checks, not slogans:

  • 1. Black balance completes per the menu with the lens capped.
  • 2. Dark-area footage exists before and after for comparison.
  • 3. Shadow noise drops visibly instead of being kept as style.

Failures

Any one of these means stop and fix it before post:

  • 1. Check 1 is only staged (“Black balance completes per the menu with the…”); another camera position or location exposes it.
  • 2. Check 2 is left to post or a verbal explanation (“Dark-area footage exists before and after for…”); nothing on set can be rechecked.
  • 3. Do not skip black balance after conditions change; shadow noise drifts with temperature.

Extend

10-minute exercise: Run a 60-second test with the gear you already own, measuring only “Cap the lens and run black balance from”. Record two numbers: what you need and what the gear actually gives.

Check

  • Black balance completes per the menu with the lens capped.
  • Dark-area footage exists before and after for comparison.
  • Shadow noise drops visibly instead of being kept as style.
