AI previz and storyboard

Previz is not a pretty first render; it validates camera, pace, light and edit at the lowest cost. AI previz only needs to be judgeable, not deliverable.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Projects validating camera, pace and light before generation.
  • Clients/directors needing a moving storyboard, not text.
  • Not for: final delivery or nuanced performance/action.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Draw 6–8 boards with a shot purpose.
  2. Generate low-res/low-frame-rate previz.
  3. Validate camera/pace/light, not faces.
  4. Add temp music/VO for a 30s pass.
  5. After approval, move to high-res generation.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Making previz pretty; cost approaches final generation.
  • Fixing faces while camera/pace remain unvalidated.
  • High-res before sign-off; rework.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Previz's value is failing cheaply. A bad camera at 480p costs an hour; at 4K it costs a day. AI previz need not be pretty, only let the director/client say “wrong camera/too slow.”

A storyboard is not detail; every frame has a purpose: setup, turn, reaction, result. A frame without purpose has no edit point. Write purpose, then composition, then prompt.

Checks / 验收

  • Each board has a shot purpose.
  • Previz validates camera/pace/light only.
  • The 30s previz tells the story.
  • Formal generation only after sign-off.
