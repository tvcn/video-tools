Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • First-time users afraid of buying/using wrong.
  • Teams explaining basics to clients/colleagues.
  • Not for: legal advice or contract review.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Start with money: free/paid/API.
  2. Then rights: commercial/copyright/likeness.
  3. Then quality: hands/face/text/audio.
  4. Then delivery: ratio/duration/subtitles.
  5. When unsure, check official terms/legal.

30 questions / 30 问速查

QuestionQuestion
Is AI video free?Free tiers/trials exist; commercial use usually costs money; check terms.
Can I use it commercially?Check output rights/region/platform; contract commercial/modify/resell/exclusive.
Is AI output copyrighted?Varies by country; keep prompts/settings records and define ownership.
Can I use a celebrity face?No, unless written consent; likeness/defamation/platform risk.
Can I use a real brand logo?Only with permission; AI warps logos, composite in post.
How do I remove a watermark?Paid/API tiers usually remove it; do not bypass copyright with tools.
Why do hands fail?Small, occluded, fast; use close-up slow motion/post repair.
Why does the face change per shot?Missing character card/references; use front/profile/full + locked text.
Why is text garbled?Models are weak at type; composite text in post.
Is 4K always better?No; check subject/motion first, resolution second.
How much does 30s cost?Cost per usable second: runs×retries÷usable seconds + labor.
What GPU for self-hosting?Depends on model/quantization; 16GB entry, 24GB safer, H3 needs more.
Can open models be commercial?Check license region/revenue/attribution; open is not free commercial.
API or subscription?Compare volume/concurrency/retries; subscription for low volume, API/local for high.
What duration is best?5–10s per shot; 30s needs beats.
Can it generate dialogue?Some models have native audio; write short lines and lip-sync.
Lip-sync is off; what now?Lock mouth silent, then audio; ADR/repair in post.
How do I subtitle?SRT/ASS + post; ≤14 chars per line, bottom safe area.
How do I keep consistency?Character/set/wardrobe cards shared across shots.
How fast is generation?Depends on model/queue/resolution; batch needs a queue.
Will my data train the model?Check terms; contract no-training/deletable/no cross-border.
Which regions are blocked?Check license/platform; H3 excludes US/EU/UK/KR.
My account was banned; what now?Read rules/appeal; do not evade with multiple accounts.
Can I use references?Yes, with rights; separate character/product/set.
Can AI video deliver cinema?Usually no; resolution/bit depth/detail need real/CG.
How do I archive?Prompts/references/version/settings/failure notes together.
Model retired; what now?Export assets/prompts/project; find primary/backup and retest.
Can I batch generate?Yes; design idempotency/rate limits/retries/budget first.
How do I judge a model?Same prompt three times, six classes, two blind raters; not leaderboards.
Will AI replace editors?No; it replaces generation, not story/pace/judgment.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Treating FAQ as legal advice.
  • Treating a trial as permanently free.
  • Treating commercial as resale/exclusive.

Go deeper / 继续往下

FAQ reduces beginner mistakes; it does not replace legal. Money/account/rights answers point to official terms; quality/delivery answers give steps. Do not mix.

Beginners ask “which model is best.” The answer is “depends on shot/delivery/budget,” not a name. Break the question into options, not one universal answer.

Checks / 验收

  • Every answer is actionable or points official.
  • Legal answers say official/legal.”
  • No “forever/universal” promises.
  • Quarterly price/platform update.
