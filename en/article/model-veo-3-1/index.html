Veo 3.1: what it is good for

Veo 3.1 is built around 4–8s clips, native audio, 4K and start/end frames. It fits high-value short shots, not low-cost batch work.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • High-end product shots, key 4–8s brand beats, native dialogue/ambience.
  • Shots with a first/last frame or reference image where composition must lock.
  • Not for: 30s one-takes, long dialogue, or low-budget batch work with many retries.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Compress the shot to 4–8s with one action and one light source.
  2. Draft at 720p silent to confirm composition and motion, then enable audio/4K.
  3. Lock composition with frames, not expression; leave expression to generation.
  4. Iterate on Fast/Lite; use Pro only for final candidates.
  5. Price per second and include retries in the per-clip cost.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Using 4K for every draft; iteration cost multiplies.
  • Expecting a full arc in 8s; it fits one beat.
  • Treating channel price as official; regions and credits differ.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Veo 3.1's strength is not length but finishing picture and sound in one short shot. That fits key brand beats, product close-ups and ambience transitions; a 30s continuous performance will be pushed back to the edit.

Price by usable second: an 8s 4K clip with audio that needs three failures costs four times the list price. Iterate composition, motion and line rhythm on Fast/Lite, then run finals on Pro.

Checks / 验收

  • The shot stays within 8s with one main action.
  • Silent 720p passes composition before audio/4K.
  • Three runs with face, hands, text and audio logged.
  • Per-clip cost includes retries, not only the successful take.
