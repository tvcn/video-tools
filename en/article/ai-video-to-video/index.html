Video-to-video: style, motion, repaint

Video-to-video is not a filter; it treats existing footage as structure and repaints style, material and light. Structure fidelity, temporal consistency and reusable motion decide whether it enters post.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Existing footage needing a style, era, material or weather change.
  • Shots keeping original motion and repainting appearance.
  • Not for: frame-accurate control or full CG replacement.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Test one second: edges, occlusion, motion.
  2. Lock source timecode/frame rate; no retiming.
  3. Repaint in regions: subject/background/props.
  4. Check flicker across ten frames of one region.
  5. Check color/edges/alpha before NLE roundtrip.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Repainting the whole frame; subject edges melt.
  • Changing frame rate; motion speed wrong.
  • Not checking alpha; black edges after comp.

Go deeper / 继续往下

V2V succeeds or fails on edges and time. Models melt subject edges into the background and make fabric texture flicker. Pass edges, occlusion and motion in a one-second test before style; a good style with melted edges is unusable.

It fits appearance changes: material, era, weather, style. It does not fit actor/set/action replacement; those need roto, tracking, CG or reshoot. Classify tasks as appearance vs structure to avoid rework.

Checks / 验收

  • One-second test passes edges/occlusion/motion.
  • Timecode/frame rate match source.
  • No flicker across ten frames.
  • Color/alpha correct after roundtrip.
