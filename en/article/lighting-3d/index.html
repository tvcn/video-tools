3D Lighting: Three Points and HDRI

Use key, fill, and rim to build structure, then HDRI for environment. Reverse the order and space flattens.

3D lighting is not filling a scene with light. Set one key direction so shadows explain space; use ambient and fill to control contrast. Even a realistic material looks like a texture map when the key is wrong.

3D lighting starts with key direction, then materials

For every added light, name the real source it replaces; if none, delete it. Keep light layers separable for comparison.

Limits

HDRI is not universal. Interiors, night and hard directional light need manual lamps; an environment map alone goes flat and shadows scatter.

3D lighting: key light first, atmosphere later

The most common 3D lighting mistake is adding atmosphere before the key. Volumetrics, glow and depth of field erase direction. The order is key → fill → rim → environment → atmosphere.

LayerRoleCheck
KeySets time and directionDo shadows fall one way?
FillControls contrastShadow detail without milkiness
RimSeparates subject from backgroundEdges not blown
EnvironmentReflection and skyHDRI matches the set

Project-level checks

  1. Turn off all atmosphere and judge the silhouette on key light alone. If the silhouette fails, atmosphere cannot save it.
  2. Use one lighting rig across shots; record why a shot needs a special light.
  3. Render reflection and refraction as a separate pass so post can adjust. Baking them into beauty locks them.
  4. Check 10% gray and skin, not “brightness”; expose for the midtones.

If render time runs short, cut atmosphere passes before key-light samples. Key noise ruins the whole shot.

On set

Turn off the HDRI and shape the form with the key only. Add fill and rim, then bring the HDRI back to 20 or 30 percent for environment.

Do this

3D lighting builds structure with key, fill, and rim before HDRI; reversing the order flattens space.

  1. Turn off HDRI and shape with the key only.
  2. Add fill and rim.
  3. Bring HDRI in at 20 to 30 percent to fill the environment.

Avoid

Do not start with HDRI; environment light hides the form.

Judge

Three signals are observable on set:

  • the speed split between foreground, midground and background reads in one pan
  • a 0.5-second action still carries weight or anticipation
  • turn off any layer and the space still holds

Failures

These three moves erase the detail you just built:

  • more layers, same speed: the space stays flat
  • anticipation without follow-through: the weight is only half done
  • rendering before assets are locked: rework starts at the compositor

Extend

10-minute exercise: Draw three keys at 24 fps, change only “Add fill and rim.”, and export two seconds. If the weight still does not read, add anticipation before adding blur.

3 checks

  • With HDRI off, the key already defines form.
  • Fill and rim do their jobs without flattening the key.
  • HDRI stays at 20 to 30 percent and does not take over.
