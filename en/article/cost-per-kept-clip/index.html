Cost per usable clip calculator

Do not budget by list price per second. Use generations × price per run ÷ usable seconds, plus review and post, to get cost per usable second.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Producers: price retries and review before quoting.
  • Creators: compare subscription, API and local costs.
  • Not for: procurement comparing only successful-run price.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Write target finished seconds, excluding titles/end cards.
  2. Record price per candidate and average retries.
  3. Record usable rate: how many of ten enter the edit.
  4. Add review minutes and post minutes.
  5. Total cost ÷ usable seconds = cost per usable second.

Parameters / 参数与判断

项目 / Item本页口径 / This page
公式
Formula		(生成次数 × 单次价格 + 人工 + 后期) ÷ 可用秒数
(runs × price + labor + post) ÷ usable seconds
示例 A
Example A		$0.40/秒 × 8 秒 × 4 次 = $12.80；可用 8 秒 → $1.60/秒
$0.40/s × 8s × 4 runs = $12.80; 8 usable seconds → $1.60/s
示例 B
Example B		$0.07/秒 × 15 秒 × 2 次 = $2.10；可用 10 秒 → $0.21/秒
$0.07/s × 15s × 2 = $2.10; 10 usable seconds → $0.21/s
人工
Labor		按小时费率 × 筛选/后期分钟
Hourly rate × review/post minutes
别漏
Do not omit		失败重试、订阅积分、存储/电费
Retries, credits, storage/power

Avoid / 别踩

  • Multiplying the successful take's per-second price; missing retries.
  • Treating subscription credits as free; top-ups cost extra.
  • Treating review as zero; lower usable rate means higher labor.

Go deeper / 继续往下

The easiest self-deception is “one 8s 4K clip is $4.40, so 30s costs $16.50.” Reality: 3–4 runs per shot, 3 of 10 usable, 30s can exceed $100. Put retries and usable rate into the formula.

Local deployment is not free either: GPU depreciation, power, ops, downloads and reruns. Put cloud API and local GPU in one table and compare usable minutes per month, not tokens or GPU hours.

Checks / 验收

  • Formula includes generation, retries, labor and post.
  • At least two models/plans compared.
  • Usable rate comes from ten real samples, not a guess.
  • Quote states the cost-per-usable-second basis.
