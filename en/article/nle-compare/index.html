Choosing an NLE: Start With the Project

Choose an NLE by project needs: collaboration, proxies, grading, subtitles, delivery; not by tutorial count.

3 min read · Field noteTVCN Tools DeskEdited 2026-11

If the team is fluent and delivering on time, switching editors resets muscle memory. Optimize proxies, cache and project structure in the current tool first.

Choose by project, not reputation

  • Media and formats: confirm native support for your camera formats, proxies and color management.
  • Collaboration: shared projects, media management and version control; many single-user features do not make a team tool.
  • Delivery: subtitles, masters, audio and platform outputs; do not discover a missing feature at delivery.

When not to switch

On set

Cut one minute of the same footage in three apps. Compare proxies, collaboration, subtitles, and export before choosing.

Do this

Choose an NLE by project: collaboration, proxies, grading, subtitles, delivery; not by tutorial count.

  1. Cut one minute of the same footage in three apps.
  2. Compare proxies, collaboration, subtitles, and export.
  3. Decide by the project's weakest requirement.

Avoid

Do not choose by tutorial volume; if the project lacks collaboration, tutorials cannot fix it.

3 checks

  • Proxies, collaboration, subtitles, and export are compared.
  • The choice follows the project's weakest need, not popularity.
