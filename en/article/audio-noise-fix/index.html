Audio Noise Fix: Start With the Room

Start with the room: air conditioning, lights, outlet, cable; blame the mic last.

2 min read · Field noteTVCN Tools DeskEdited 2026-11

Classify noise before choosing a tool

Three kinds: constant floor (AC, fan), intermittent (door, car), voice flaws (sibilance, plosives). Constant floor can be reduced; intermittent must be cut or re-recorded; sibilance uses EQ and de-essing, not global denoise.

Keep an untouched track; after processing, listen for muffled or metallic dialogue. A little floor is better than a robot voice.

Limits

Denoise cannot rescue unintelligible dialogue. If speech is mushy, re-record or dub; denoise makes mush more obvious.

On set

Turn off air conditioning, lights, and fans, then change outlets and cables. Record a comparison on a phone to find the source.

Do this

Noise starts in the room: turn off AC, change outlets and cables, blame the mic last.

  1. Turn off AC, fridge, and lights; record thirty seconds of silence.
  2. Change outlets and cables, and put gain on the recorder.
  3. Move the subject closer and record room tone for a bed.

Avoid

Do not buy a new mic first; the noise may be power or the room.

3 checks

  • Thirty seconds of silence was recorded with AC, fridge, and lights off.
  • Outlets and cables were changed and gain sits on the recorder.
  • The subject is close to the mic and room tone exists.
