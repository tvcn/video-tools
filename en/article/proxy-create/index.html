Proxy Creation: Match the Timeline

Generate proxies to match timeline and delivery; full-res editing makes subtitles and framing wrong.

Do not rename files or change folder structure when generating proxies. The editor relinks by original name and timecode; renaming turns relinking into manual hunting.

Proxies keep names, timecode and reel

Pick proxy resolution from timeline needs: multicam 4K can go to 1080p or 720p, but frame rate and color space must match the original.

Limits

A proxy is not delivery. Before the master, confirm the timeline points at originals; exporting from proxies loses a generation.

Proxy specs: editable, relinkable, never the master

A proxy is not “just a small file.” It must relink at the same timecode, frame rate and filename, or the cut shifts when you return to the original.

  1. Common: 1080p ProRes Proxy or high-bitrate H.264, same frame rate as source, audio kept.
  2. Add _proxy to the filename; do not change reel names. Relink against the original media path.
  3. Keep originals separate from the proxy project. Never grade the proxy as final.

Acceptance: pick a random cut point and relink to the original; the frame should not shift and audio should stay in sync. If it shifts, check frame rate and timecode.

On set

Make quarter-resolution ProRes Proxy files on a separate SSD. Relink and check timecode and framing.

Do this

Proxies follow timeline and delivery resolution; do not cut at full resolution. Wrong proxies misplace subtitles and framing.

  1. Generate quarter-resolution ProRes Proxy.
  2. Keep them on a separate SSD.
  3. On relink, check timecode and framing.

Avoid

Do not export from proxies; relinking to originals shifts every subtitle.

Judge

This page passes on three observable checks, not slogans:

  • 1. Proxies are quarter-resolution ProRes Proxy on a separate SSD.
  • 2. Timeline and proxy names map back to originals.
  • 3. After relink, timecode and framing match the proxy cut.

Failures

Any one of these means stop and fix it before post:

  • 1. Check 1 is only staged (“Proxies are quarter-resolution ProRes Proxy on…”); another camera position or location exposes it.
  • 2. Check 2 is left to post or a verbal explanation (“Timeline and proxy names map back to originals”); nothing on set can be rechecked.
  • 3. Do not export from proxies; relinking to originals shifts every subtitle.

Extend

10-minute exercise: Run a 60-second test with the gear you already own, measuring only “Generate quarter-resolution ProRes Proxy.”. Record two numbers: what you need and what the gear actually gives.
