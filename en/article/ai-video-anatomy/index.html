The six parts of an AI video

An AI video is not one prompt; it is six parts: shot, subject, action, light, sound and edit. Miss one and the viewer says something is off.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Beginners who can generate a clip but the finished piece feels fake.
  • Producers explaining what an AI video actually consists of.
  • Not for: people wanting a one-click template.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Write the shot: size, angle, lens.
  2. Write the subject: age, wardrobe, material, state.
  3. Write one action with a start and end.
  4. Write light: direction, hardness, temperature.
  5. Add voice/SFX/subtitles last, but plan sound early.

Parameters / 参数与判断

项目 / Item本页口径 / This page
镜头
Shot		中景/近景/特写 + 平视/低机位 + 24–50mm
Medium/close/wide + eye/low + 24–50mm
主体
Subject		一个人/一个产品，别塞三个主角
One person/product, not three heroes
动作
Action		5 秒内只发生一件事
One event in five seconds
光
Light		一个主光方向 + 一个补光
One key direction + one fill
声音
Sound		对白/环境/音乐三层，先规划后铺
Dialogue/ambience/music planned before mixing

Avoid / 别踩

  • Writing two of six parts and letting the model guess.
  • Using “cinematic” as a substitute for light, lens and grade.
  • Planning sound last; lip-sync, subtitles and music collide.

Go deeper / 继续往下

“Fake” is usually missing parts, not a weak model: no light on the face, no weight in motion, no depth in the background, no ambience in sound. Fill the six parts before buying a more expensive model.

Set a minimum for each part: shot names its size, subject names three features, action names start/end, light names direction, sound has three layers, edit has pace. Pass six, then talk style.

Checks / 验收

  • Each of the six parts fits one sentence.
  • One 5s shot has one main action.
  • You can point to the light direction in frame.
  • All three sound layers exist and do not mask each other.
