Native audio and dialogue

Native audio is not automatic mixing; dialogue, lip-sync, ambience and SFX are generated together. Write line rhythm first, let the model speak, then repair in post rather than rebuild.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Talking heads, dialogue drama and product explainers needing sync sound.
  • Projects avoiding a full dub/lip-sync pass.
  • Not for: multilingual dubbing, musicals or complex mixes.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Rewrite lines short; mark comma/period pauses.
  2. Write numbers/abbreviations as spoken words.
  3. Lock mouth position silent, then enable audio.
  4. Keep ambience as a separate layer.
  5. Mix: dialogue around -12 LUFS, music 6–10 dB under.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Writing long written sentences; the model breaks them wrong.
  • Enabling audio before final lines; lip-sync fails.
  • Generating ambience with dialogue; cannot fix separately.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Native audio promises no dubbing and hides a trap: bad lines. The model follows punctuation but not meaning; put keywords in short lines and pauses in punctuation, more useful than a better voice.

Lip-sync repair order is consonants, vowels, expression. Wrong b/p/m closure is instantly visible; vowel length matters less; expression last. Fix dialogue, then ambience, then music.

Checks / 验收

  • Lines are short; numbers/abbreviations read correctly.
  • Mouth position aligns in the silent pass.
  • Ambience has its own track.
  • Phone speaker: dialogue clear, music not masking.
