AI Replacement: Do Not Swap the Face

AI can replace background, weather, or crowd. Do not casually replace an actor's face.

Replacement preserves light and motion

AI replacement of a person, background or prop fails at light direction and inertia. Shoot the original light, shadow and motion first, then generate only the material or region; do not regenerate the whole frame.

Use a difference check on edges: hair, fingers, transparent objects and motion blur show first.

Limits

Replacement cannot change facts. No generative replacement in news, documentary or evidence; ads and fiction may use it with disclosure.

AI face/voice replacement: consent before quality

The floor for face and voice replacement is not likeness; it is whether the person agreed and whether the viewer knows. No real person without consent, no deceased person, no minor. If you do it, you cannot publish it.

Project-level checks

  1. The release states media, territory, term, sublicensing and withdrawal.
  2. Put a “synthetic image” label in the first three seconds and in the caption.
  3. Keep source, generation record and human review; if disputed, pull it down fast.
  4. If the client asks for “undetectable AI,” refuse. Concealment is the risk.

Do not do face swaps for news, politics, finance or medical content. The cost of mistaken identity is far above the production fee.

On set

Replace background, weather, and extras only. Keep the lead actor's real face with masks and tracking; do not generate a new face.

Do this

AI replacement can change background, weather, and crowd; do not replace the actor's face casually.

  1. Change only background, weather, and extras.
  2. Keep the lead's face from the plate and use masks and tracking.
  3. Do not generate a full face without written permission and clear labeling.

Avoid

Do not swap the lead's face casually; the audience knows that face and exits the scene.

3 checks

  • Only background, weather, or extras change; the lead's face remains live-action.
  • Masks and tracking keep the face edge stable.
  • No full-face generation without written permission.
