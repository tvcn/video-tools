Buy the Lens First: Focal Length, Aperture, Breathing

The lens decides half the image and holds value better than a body. Buy one focal length that covers 70% of your work, then a second; do not start with three cheap zooms.

First decision: which link is missing

List the focal lengths in your last 20 films. If 35mm and 50mm dominate, buy a fast 35 or 50 first. If you shoot events and cannot move, consider a 24-70 zoom. The first lens should not exceed 60% of the budget; leave money for a second lens or light.

Fits:

  • Portrait/interview, shallow depth
  • Low-light interiors, no light
  • One lens to build a look

Does not fit:

  • Events/sports needing fast zoom
  • Product macro at 1:1
  • One ultra-wide for everything

Test conditions: how to check before buying

This is not a cross-brand shootout and it does not settle brands. These three checks are a field protocol you can repeat in a shop or with a loaner; cells without measurements say “not measured” rather than inventing numbers. Record body, firmware, room temperature, recording format and card; change one condition and the result can change.

Pass line: Breathing does not reframe, edges are usable, and close focus covers hands or product detail.

If one of the three can only be answered by “I heard” or a spec sheet, do not order yet. Write the condition into your shoot plan, then decide whether it changes your kit.

Test protocol: reproducible, no invented scores

Bring the body and shoot three frames: a face, a hand detail, and a backlit window. Check edges, chromatic aberration, focus breathing, and vignetting—not only centre sharpness.

Alternatives and upgrades: do not add money on the wrong axis

  • Rent three, buy after
  • Used OEM prime
  • Add light instead of glass

Buying steps: bring footage, not spec sheets

Run these in order before paying. If one fails, do not tell yourself post will fix it.

  1. List focal lengths from 20 films
  2. Shoot face/hand/backlight
  3. Compare breathing and close focus
  4. Check filters and hood
  5. Check warranty/used glass

Regional note for US / UK / EU readers

This English page keeps mainland-China examples where they are part of the method. Read platform names as examples, not as the only market: Douyin ↔ TikTok/Reels, Bilibili ↔ YouTube, Xiaohongshu ↔ Instagram/Pinterest; RMB figures are not converted here. Voltage, plug, warranty, import duty and radio certification differ by market; check the local spec sheet before ordering gear or batteries.

3 checks

  • The last three months of shooting types are counted.
  • A 24-70 and a 35 were borrowed for a day each and used focal lengths logged.
  • The chosen lens covers eighty percent of the work.
