Runway Gen-4.5: camera control and workflow

Gen-4.5 is a 2–10s, 720p text/image-to-video model strong in camera choreography, sequenced actions and Aleph 2.0 editing. It is not a direct 4K delivery source.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Shots needing precise camera paths, continuous action and previz.
  • Projects using Aleph 2.0 for local edits or style continuation.
  • Not for: direct 4K delivery, 30s one-takes, dialogue-led projects.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Draw the camera path: start, pass, stop, then write it.
  2. Split action into 2–3 beats of 2–4s each.
  3. Use Gen-4.5 for 720p previz to lock framing and motion.
  4. Use Aleph 2.0 for local changes instead of rerunning.
  5. For 4K, upscale and check sharpening and noise.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Treating 720p as a 4K master; upscaled detail is synthetic.
  • Putting a whole scene in one prompt; it choreographs, not long-form narrates.
  • Ignoring credits; per-second price is not the plan cost.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Runway's difference is not a prettier clip but camera motion as a choreographed path. “Orbit” is useless; “push from the door to one meter from the table, hold two seconds, then truck right 30cm” is useful. Gen-4.5 responds more reliably to paths and sequenced action than adjectives.

Gen-4.5 plus Aleph 2.0 fits a “shoot then revise” flow: lock motion at 720p previz, change locally in Aleph, then upscale for delivery. Do not treat it as a 4K generator; treat it as previz and an edit layer.

Checks / 验收

  • Camera path draws as a line with start, pass, stop.
  • Each shot is 2–10s with 2–3 action beats.
  • 720p previz passes before Aleph/upscale.
  • 4K output checked for sharpening halos, noise and title safe.
