AI Video / Scenario

News and documentary

In news/documentary, AI may do explanatory reconstruction, never fake a scene. Every AI image must be labeled and cannot replace real interviews, people or events.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • News explainers, historical/scientific reconstruction, data visualisation.
  • Documentary projects needing clear AI labels.
  • Not for: faking scenes, impersonating real people, reenacting crime.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Ask: is there real footage? If yes, use it.
  2. Without footage, do explanatory reconstruction, not event reenactment.
  3. Label every AI image with corner mark + VO/subtitle.
  4. Use composites/silhouettes, not recognizable real faces.
  5. Editor/legal review facts and labels before publish.

Avoid / 别踩

  • AI reenacts crime/disaster; ethical/legal risk.
  • Unlabeled AI; viewers take it as real.
  • Generating a real face; likeness/defamation risk.

Go deeper / 继续往下

News底线: viewers must know what was captured and what is explanation. AI reconstruction is not the sin; unlabeled is. Corner, VO and subtitle labeling is the minimum, not a bonus.

Historical/scientific reconstruction can use AI but must be grounded in verifiable sources: date, place, clothing, objects, maps. The model does not know 1920s Shanghai; it generates “old-looking” images. Check details by hand.

Checks / 验收

  • Real footage not replaced by AI.
  • Every AI image has triple labeling.
  • No recognizable real face.
  • Editor/legal review recorded.
