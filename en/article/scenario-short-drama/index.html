Short drama and multi-shot narrative

Short drama is not a shortened film; it is a hook every 15s and a turn per episode. AI shoots clips, but character/set/wardrobe continuity depends on cards.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Vertical short drama, branded drama, serialized stories.
  • Projects with 3–10 episodes of 60–120s.
  • Not for: feature films or complex action/crowds.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Write one episode: hook/conflict/turn/cliffhanger.
  2. Character/set/wardrobe cards shared across three episodes.
  3. Two or three shots per scene, not twenty.
  4. End each episode with one open question.
  5. Pilot one episode; scale only after data.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Twenty shots per episode; generation/edit collapse.
  • Face changes every episode; viewers drop.
  • No cliffhanger; no reason to watch next.

Go deeper / 继续往下

Short drama's AI cost is continuity, not generation. Twenty shots × three takes = 60 generations; one character drift reruns the episode. Cut to 8–12 shots and lock cards; cost halves.

Short drama pace is information density, not fast cuts. Every 15s a new fact: identity, secret, threat, turn. Picture can be slow; information cannot stop. Beauty without new information gets swiped.

Checks / 验收

  • 60–120s with a hook every 15s.
  • Character/wardrobe/set consistent across three episodes.
  • Cliffhanger per episode.
  • Pilot one episode before scaling.
