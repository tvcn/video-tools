Brand Film Template: Stance, Evidence, Landing

A usable 45-second segment table

SegmentTimePictureSound / pass line
Claim0–5sA concrete scene, no logo yetOne arguable sentence under 15 words
Proof 15–15sOne person doing one specific thingAction sound; no “craftsmanship” voiceover
Proof 215–25sA difficulty appears, the person does not retreatOne cost line; no slogan
Counter-example25–35sAnother way fails or is rejectedDo not mock competitors; show the difference
Landing35–45sThe person acts; brand appears lastEnd on action, not a slogan

Fill three lines before rolling

  • Claim: write a sentence a counter-example could challenge; if it cannot be opposed, it is not a claim.
  • Proof: one person and one action, not “team spirit.”
  • Landing: what action should remain after the film?

On set

The claim must be arguable, the proof must be one person, and the landing must be an action. No dreams or craftsmanship.

Do this

A brand-film template uses claim, evidence, landing; the claim must be opposable and the evidence specific.

  1. Write a claim someone could oppose.
  2. Prove it with one specific person.
  3. Land on an action, not a slogan.

Avoid

Do not use dream or craftsmanship as the claim; without evidence they are only words.

3 checks

  • The claim can be opposed and a counterexample exists.
  • Evidence is specific to one person and one action.
  • The ending lands on an action, not a slogan.
