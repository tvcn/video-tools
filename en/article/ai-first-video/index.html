AI Video / Beginner

Your first 30-second AI video

Seven days is not learning every model. It is finishing one 30s postable video: three shots, one music bed, one subtitle set, one phone check.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • First AI video; you need a complete seven-day path.
  • You want a 30s piece to validate model, edit and subtitles.
  • Not for: commercial delivery, multi-person teams or licensed talent.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Day 1: one topic sentence, one audience, one delivery line.
  2. Day 2: draw three shots with subject/action/camera/light.
  3. Day 3–4: run three takes per shot; keep the best.
  4. Day 5: rough-cut to 30s; fix pace first.
  5. Day 6: subtitles, music, loudness; Day 7 phone check.

Avoid / 别踩

  • Switching models on day three; finish three takes first.
  • Finding subtitles cover the product on day five; leave safe area at generation.
  • Filling with music first; dialogue disappears and completion drops.

Go deeper / 继续往下

The assignment is completion, not perfection. On day three you will want to change model, script and music; resist and finish the three shots. Only a finished piece shows whether failure is in writing, generation, editing or subtitles.

Do the day-seven phone check for real: vertical, speaker, 50% brightness. If the subject is unclear, subtitles too small or music masks dialogue, fix it; do not fool yourself on a desktop monitor.

Checks / 验收

  • One idea in 30s.
  • All three shots read on a phone.
  • Subtitles ≤14 characters and do not cover face/product.
  • On phone speaker, dialogue is clear and music does not mask it.

Regional note for US / UK / EU readers

This English page keeps mainland-China examples where they are part of the method. Read platform names as examples, not as the only market: Douyin ↔ TikTok/Reels, Bilibili ↔ YouTube, Xiaohongshu ↔ Instagram/Pinterest; RMB figures are not converted here. Model availability, training-data rules, disclosure duties and privacy law differ by market; check the EU AI Act, UK GDPR and your local platform policy before publishing.
