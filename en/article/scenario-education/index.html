AI Video / Scenario

Education and explainer video

Knowledge video must be correct first, clear second. AI fits demo animation, analogy scenes and transitions; it should not replace the teacher on core concepts.

Fit and no-fit / 适用与不适用

  • Courses, science explainers, product training, paid knowledge.
  • Projects needing demo animation/analogy scenes without full animation.
  • Not for: regulated medical/legal/financial advice.

Do this / 怎么做

  1. Write a teaching script: one concept, one example, one check question.
  2. Turn the abstract concept into one visual analogy.
  3. AI for demo/transitions; keep teacher/handwriting/objects.
  4. Add subtitles/glossary/chapter markers together.
  5. Check whether viewers can restate, not only completion.

Avoid / 别踩

  • AI-generated formulas/charts; symbols wrong.
  • Pretty animation hides unclear teaching; high completion, low learning.
  • AI explaining regulated advice; compliance risk.

Go deeper / 继续往下

AI-flavored edu video is full picture, empty information. One concept with one analogy, one example and one check question beats ten showy animations. AI makes the analogy, not reads the slides.

Formulas, numbers, maps and historical figures must be human-checked. Models write π as 3.14, wrong dates and wrong maps; these errors matter more than looks.

Checks / 验收

  • One core concept per video.
  • Formulas/charts/terms human-checked.
  • Restate/check question, not only completion.
  • Regulated content not freely generated.
