Renting Lighting: List and Damage

List, test, and photograph the gear; one missing soft box costs an hour on set.

Draw the positions: key, fill, rim, background. For each, decide size, color temperature and diffusion. Rent by position, not “more is better.”

Rent by lighting roles, not lamp count

Test each lamp at pickup for color, output, flicker and accessories; shoot a phone clip for banding.

Limits

More lamps is not better light. Two fighting from one position lose to one lamp and a diffusion frame. Get direction right, then quantity.

On set

List every light, softbox, stand, sandbag, and cable. Test each at pickup and video the condition.

Do this

Light rental: list every item, test every unit, and record damage. One missing softbox costs an hour on set.

  1. List lights, softboxes, stands, sandbags, cables, and gels.
  2. Power up each unit and test color temperature and output.
  3. Video the condition and accessories; compare before returning.

Avoid

Do not count heads only; one missing softbox or mount stops the shoot.

Judge

This page passes on three observable checks, not slogans:

  • 1. The list covers lights, softboxes, stands, sandbags, cables, and gels.
  • 2. Every unit was powered up and tested for color temperature and output.
  • 3. Condition and accessories are videoed and checked before return.

Failures

Any one of these means stop and fix it before post:

  • 1. Check 1 is only staged (“The list covers lights, softboxes, stands, san…”); another camera position or location exposes it.
  • 2. Check 2 is left to post or a verbal explanation (“Every unit was powered up and tested for color…”); nothing on set can be rechecked.
  • 3. Do not count heads only; one missing softbox or mount stops the shoot.

Extend

10-minute exercise: Run a 60-second test with the gear you already own, measuring only “List lights, softboxes, stands, sandbags, cables, and gels.”. Record two numbers: what you need and what the gear actually gives.

Check

  • The list covers lights, softboxes, stands, sandbags, cables, and gels.
  • Every unit was powered up and tested for color temperature and output.
  • Condition and accessories are videoed and checked before return.
