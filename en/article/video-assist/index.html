Video Assist: Playback on Set

Video assist handles playback, marks, and backup; instant review speeds the whole set.

3 min read · Field noteTVCN Tools DeskEdited 2026-11

  2. When not to use it

  5. Avoid
  6. 3 checks

On a small crew with one camera and the director beside the monitor, a video assistant is an extra role; solve monitor position and communication first.

What to compare first

  • Latency and playback: confirm camera-to-monitor delay and instant playback; a three-second wait breaks the director's rhythm.
  • LUT and recording: LUT support, clean feed and backup recording; a pretty image is not enough.
  • Power and cable: long runs and strain relief; a loose connector is more common than a missing feature.

When not to use it

On set

Mark every take good or bad with scene and shot number, and back up the card so the director can replay without waiting.

Do this

Video assist handles playback, marking, and backup; when the director can see the last take immediately, the set moves faster.

  1. Mark good, bad, scene, and shot for every take.
  2. Back up the card.
  3. Playback reaches the right take without waiting.

Avoid

Do not back up without marking; when the director asks for playback, the set waits while you search.

3 checks

  • Every take carries good/bad, scene, and shot marks.
  • Cards are backed up between takes with verification.
  • A requested take plays within thirty seconds.
