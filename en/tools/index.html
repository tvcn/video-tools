AI Video / Professional PostVideo-to-video: style, motion, repaintVideo-to-video is not a filter; it treats existing footage as structure and repaints style, material and light. Structure fidelity, temporal consistency and reusable motion decide whether it enters post. AI Video / Professional PostAI cleanup and set extensionCleanup starts with a rebuildable background. Remove pedestrians, wires or mistakes only with a clean plate, tracking and occlusion; AI is not a magic eraser. AI Video / Professional PostAI color and relightAI color is not a LUT and relight is not brightness. Unify color management, relight by region, protect skin; otherwise faces turn plastic and backgrounds gray. AI Video / Professional PostAI audio postAI audio post is not one-click denoise. Order: dialogue repair → ADR → Foley → mix → loudness; check each step, never enable all at once. AI Video / Professional PostAI post integrationAI output enters post through five compatibility checks: frame rate, color, alpha, timecode and metadata. Miss one and the roundtrip drops frames, shifts color or adds black edges. AI Video / Professional PostDelivery specsDelivery is not one MP4. Platform, broadcast and ad versions differ in ratio, bitrate, loudness, subtitle safe area and naming; set delivery before generation. AI Video / ReferenceSoftware, hardware and budgetThe bottleneck is often RAM/storage/network, not GPU. Prevent out-of-memory, lost assets and slow upload before chasing speed. AI Video / ReferencePlatform specsPlatform specs are not permanent; this page gives November 2026 recommendations: 9:16 1080×1920, 15/30/60s, H.264, -14 LUFS, bottom 15% safe. AI Video / Models & CostCost per usable clip calculatorDo not budget by list price per second. Use generations × price per run ÷ usable seconds, plus review and post, to get cost per usable second.
